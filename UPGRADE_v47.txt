G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 47 (2026-11-02)
=================================================================

The requests of ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_WATER_COOP_SYSTEMS_v17.xlsx. This package holds only
the files added or changed since version 46. Copy the folder over your installation,
keeping the folder structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
No migration this time: no table, column, page or setting is added, and no amount
changes. database/accounting_system.sql and accounting_system_clean.sql are unchanged
(they are not in the package). Works on MySQL 5.5 as before.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser.

What changed (see docs/ADDITIONAL_MODULE_V17_CHECKLIST.md)
----------------------------------------------------------
- Billing Adjustments > Edit opens the bill itself, instead of the small "Edit
  Uncollected Bill" box. The bill is shown as it is printed: the cooperative, the
  bill's kind and invoice number, its status, who it is billed to (account number,
  member or customer, address), its dates, term, period and class, then
    a water bill:        the meter reading (previous, present, consumption), the rate
                         code and pipe size, the tariff lines (range, rate, m3, amount),
                         the free cubic meters and privilege discount, the amount due;
    a one-time bill:     the bill code and description, the quantity, the unit price,
                         the amount, the gross, discount and total, the balance due;
    an installment bill: the same, with its payment schedule;
    a recurring bill:    the same, with its service period.
  The fields that can be corrected are boxes on the bill itself (the present reading of
  a water bill; the quantity of a material, which is priced from its bill code; the
  amount of any other charge, on the Total amount line when a discount comes off the
  bill; the due date of a one-time or recurring bill). Until something is changed the
  bill shows what it was issued for; as you type it is worked out again on the server,
  with the same calculation the save uses (reading only, nothing is written): the tariff
  lines, the amount, the schedule of an installment bill, the term and the
  disconnection date of a new due date. Beside the bill: the amount now and corrected,
  by how much it goes up or down (and, when saving it unchanged would price it
  otherwise at today's tariff or price, that amount), the class and the reason, Save
  correction and Cancel.
  The rules are as before: only a bill nothing has been collected on, a Super
  Administrator only, the journal entry voided and a correction entry "-R1" posted (a
  reading not yet posted only changes). A bill that can no longer be edited opens read
  only, with the reason. A refused correction comes back to the bill with what was typed.
  Corrections of the rules: a due date before the bill date is refused (it was
  accepted); the term (days) and the disconnection date follow a corrected due date
  (they kept their old values), the term of a recurring bill counted from the end of its
  service period, as when it is generated; a due date left as it is keeps the bill's
  due date, term and disconnection date.

- Individual Statement of Account. Generate SOA / Post Billing has two tabs, Post
  Billing and Individual Statement of Account; Reports > Generate Statement of Account
  has the statement of one billing period and the Individual Statement of Account.
    1. Member or customer: type a name, an account number or a member number (from one
       letter; Show: All, Members only, Customers only). Each member or customer is one
       name with its water-service accounts, how many unpaid invoices it has and their
       total. Someone with two accounts (two houses) gets all of them on the statement,
       or the one chosen in Accounts.
    2. Statement date (today by default).
    3. Every invoice of the member or customer that is still unpaid is listed, whatever
       its billing period: water bills, one-time bills, each unpaid amortization of an
       installment bill, recurring bills and Fixed Share Capital bills, dated on or
       before the statement date, with the amount, what was paid, the balance and
       whether it is overdue (by how many days), due today or not yet due. Tick the ones
       the statement shows: all are ticked at first except installment amortizations not
       yet due (upcoming dues, as on the statement of one billing period); Select all,
       Overdue only, Clear. A Fixed Share Capital bill is on the member's first account
       only, so it is never listed twice.
  Prepare statement prints the Statement of Account of the invoices ticked: the
  cooperative, the member or customer and the account, each invoice (date, invoice no.,
  description and period, due date, amount, paid, balance), the totals, the amount not
  yet due, due on the statement date and overdue, the total amount due, and the General
  Manager. Print statement (or Ctrl+P) prints it alone (A4 landscape). The amounts are read again
  from the bills when the statement is prepared; an invoice paid in the meantime is left
  off and said so.

- The "•" separators of the statement of one billing period and of the Post Billing
  period list are now commas.

The pages work on a phone: the list of Billing Adjustments, the bill (its tables one
line per field), the invoices to tick and the statement are stacked to the screen.
