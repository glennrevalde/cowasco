G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 52 (2026-11-07)
=================================================================

The request of ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_WATER_COOP_SYSTEMS_v18.xlsx. This package holds only
the files changed since version 51. Copy the folder over your installation, keeping the
folder structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed. No migration:
nothing to run on the database. Works on MySQL 5.5 and MySQL 8 as before.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser.

What changed (see docs/ADDITIONAL_MODULE_V18_CHECKLIST.md)
----------------------------------------------------------
- Member Center > a member > Profile > Customer Defaults has a Price level, as the
  customer form has. It decides what the member pays for the items:
    - invoices, sales receipts, sales orders, estimates, statement charges and batch
      invoices: the item's price comes from it when the member is chosen;
    - the POS: with the member as the sale's customer, the items are priced at it and
      "Price level: ..." is shown under the customer.
  A level with a price of its own for an item gives that price; otherwise its percentage
  applies to the item's sales price. Left blank, the member pays the members' price level
  of the line of business (or of the cooperative) when one is set ("Members' price
  level"), else the standard prices ("Standard"). Only an active level can be chosen; a
  level the member already has, made inactive since, is kept and shown "(inactive)".
  The change is in the audit trail.
- Water bills are not affected: a material on a water bill keeps the price of its bill
  code.
