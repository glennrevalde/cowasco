G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 27 (2026-11-15)
=================================================================

The program is renamed G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System (formerly G-SMaRT
Accounting). This package holds only the files that were added or changed since
version 26. Copy the cooperative_system folder over your installation, keeping the
folder structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
Run ONE new migration on your database (phpMyAdmin: select the database > Import;
Navicat: Execute SQL File), after backing it up:

    database/migrations/2026_11_15_multipurpose_party.sql

It adds the Party Master and the lines of business:
- new tables: parties, party_crosswalk, party_role_log, party_match_reviews,
  business_units, document_reissues;
- new columns, empty or zero at first: party_id on customers, vendors, members and
  other_names; party_id, business_unit_id and the printed-name snapshot (cp_role,
  cp_name, cp_address, cp_tin, cp_member_no, cp_source, cp_issued_at,
  cp_reissue_no) on transactions; business_unit_id on journal_lines;
  accounts.is_interunit; items.patronage_eligible; price_levels.valid_from and
  valid_to;
- the backfill: every member, customer, vendor and other name gets a party of its
  own (a customer's jobs and a member's ledger name join it); nothing is merged by
  name; the old IDs are kept in the crosswalk; every document gets its party and the
  name it prints, taken from its master as it is now;
- two lines of business, SC Savings and Credit (the cooperative vouchers) and TR
  Consumer Store (sales, purchasing, POS and inventory); banking documents and
  journal entries stay without a line of business until you assign them;
- the Parties menu, the Lines of Business page and the Statement of Operations by
  Line of Business, page rights for the cooperative roles, and four special
  permissions (see the manual, chapter 14, Rights and special permissions).

Nothing is deleted, no posted amount changes and no document number changes.
Written for MySQL 5.5, safe to run twice. With several companies, run it on every
company database (Super Admin: Companies > Database updates > Update all
companies). Until it is run, the program shows a page naming it.

database/accounting_system.sql and accounting_system_clean.sql (new installs only)
include the change and now create the database `cooperative_system`, the name in
config/config.php. Do NOT import those two files over a live database.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the new
assets/js/app.js and assets/css/app.css.

After the upgrade
-----------------
1. Cooperative > Lines of Business: rename or add the lines the cooperative runs,
   choose who may transact in each (everyone, members only, nonmembers with
   approval), the member and nonmember price levels, the default line of each kind
   of document, and switch off the modules the cooperative does not use.
2. Mark the goods and services whose member purchases or deliveries count for
   patronage refund (Lines of Business, or Inventory > Item List). After the
   upgrade no item counts until it is marked.
3. Parties > Identity Review > Look for duplicates now: decide each possible pair
   (Same or Different). A person recorded as both a member and a store customer is
   linked here, by a user with the permission to link parties, with a reason.
4. If the units lend to or charge each other, mark the inter-unit accounts on Lines
   of Business so the whole-cooperative statements leave them out.

What is new (the requests of "Multipurpose_Cooperative_Accounting_Improvement_Prompt")
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Party Master: one record and one Party ID for each person or organization,
    with its roles (member, customer, supplier, employee, other name), each role
    keeping its own number, terms, limits and status. Party Center, role history,
    give, take away, restore and move a role, make a party inactive.
 2. Possible duplicates are caught when a customer, supplier, member or party is
    added (same TIN, e-mail, mobile or phone number, name and date of birth, or
    name) and reviewed on Identity Review. Nothing is linked by name alone; linking
    needs the permission and a reason, and the old Party ID keeps pointing to the
    party kept.
 3. Member Information: "On file as" enrolls a customer or supplier already on file
    as a member on its own party. Customer, supplier and other-name forms pick the
    person or organization already on file.
 4. Names on documents: every document keeps the name, address, TIN and member
    number it was issued with, from the Party Master; the label follows the role
    (Bill to, Supplier, Pay to, Received from). "Correct printed name" reissues a
    document with a reason and an approval; Reissued Documents report.
 5. Posted documents are locked: changing their amounts, accounts, names, dates or
    lines needs the permission to edit posted documents and a reason; deleting a
    posted document is refused (void it instead).
 6. Lines of business on every document and ledger line; member and nonmember
    rules and prices; dated price levels; the usual inventory site of each line;
    modules switched off by line.
 7. Reports by line of business (the Line of business filter on the financial
    statements, ledgers and cooperative reports), eliminations of inter-unit
    balances and charges for the whole cooperative, the Statement of Operations by
    Line of Business, and receivables and payables in the Subsidiary vs General
    Ledger Reconciliation.
 8. Consolidated Party Statement: everything one party has with the cooperative,
    by role, under "Party owes the cooperative", "Cooperative owes the party" and
    "Member equity interest", each with opening, activity and closing balances and
    a link to every document; nothing is offset. Print or save as PDF, Excel, CSV.
 9. Patronage refund measured by income earned, by purchases or by deliveries,
    counting only the items and lines of business that count.
10. Excel export (.xlsx) on every report, next to CSV.
11. Confidential member details are masked for users without the permission on the
    party pages, Member Information and the member and party lists.
12. A modern layout that works on a phone: a one-row top bar, lists and tables that
    turn into cards, report tables whose amounts stay on screen.
