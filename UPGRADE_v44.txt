G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 44 (2026-11-02)
=================================================================

The requests of ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_WATER_COOP_SYSTEMS_v15.xlsx. This package holds only
the files added or changed since version 43. Copy the folder over your installation,
keeping the folder structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
No migration: nothing to run on the database. database/accounting_system.sql (the sample
database) is written again with a sample joint account and a sample institution;
accounting_system_clean.sql is unchanged.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser.

What changed (see docs/ADDITIONAL_MODULE_V15_CHECKLIST.md)
----------------------------------------------------------
- Members & Others > Member Center > New member > Joint account / Institution (and
  every saved joint account or institution) opens the form of the former program:
    Joint/Institution Details   Member ID, the status and type in large letters (NEW,
                                ASSOCIATE - JOINT), Membership name, Authorized
                                representatives (name, position / relation, member if
                                a member), Residence address, Office / business
                                address, Contact details (contact list, e-mail, TIN),
                                specimen signatures
    Membership Information      Passbook number with Get passbook number, Membership
                                date, Type, Status, PMES date, Cluster, Segmentation;
                                Branch, subscribed shares, par value, account officer;
                                the alert at the counter and notes
  Family and beneficiaries, Financial and Loan and savings grouping are not shown for a
  joint account or an institution (an individual keeps them). Water, Customer and
  Vendor Defaults stay. Choosing the Kind of member switches the form at once. A
  joint account or institution takes a member type of its kind.
- Get passbook number (on the joint form, and on Loan and savings grouping for an
  individual): the next number of Document Numbering > Passbook that no member has,
  taken from the series when the member is saved. A passbook number already given to
  another member is refused.
- The Type list of a joint account or institution shows the member types of its kind;
  a new one starts with Associate - Joint or Associate - Institutional. Its mobile is
  the first mobile of its contact list.

Set-up after the upgrade
------------------------
- Optional: Members & Others > Document Numbering > Passbook: the prefix, the next
  number and the number of digits of the passbook numbers (000001 at first).
