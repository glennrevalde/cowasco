G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 34 (2027-01-17)
=================================================================

The requests of ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_WATER_COOP_SYSTEMS_v6.xlsx. This package holds
only the files added or changed since version 33. Copy the folder over your
installation, keeping the folder structure. config/config.php is not included and
is never changed.

Database
--------
Run ONE new migration on your database (phpMyAdmin: select the database > Import;
Navicat: Execute SQL File; or php tools/upgrade.php), after backing it up:

    database/migrations/2027_01_17_passbook_designer.sql

It:
- adds passbook_types.ledger: a passbook type is a savings passbook or a loan
  passbook (a type a loan product uses becomes a loan passbook);
- adds passbook_types.layout_json: the design of the passbook (cover, header,
  body, footer), saved on Cooperative > Passbook Types;
- gives Start Line Skip and No. of Line Skip their meaning in the former
  program: the first line never printed on (the centre fold) and how many lines
  are skipped. Printing always starts on line 1. A type that listed its fold
  lines ("Lines never printed on", e.g. 13,14) gets Start Line Skip 13 and No. of
  Line Skip 2;
- gives the "Loan Passbook" installed with the program, if it was never changed,
  the set-up of the former program's loan passbook: Start Line Skip 13, No. of
  Line Skip 2, Max Lines 29, Body Left 1, a page of 4.75 x 6.75 inches, the
  footer below the last line.

Nothing is deleted, no posted amount changes, the lines already printed keep their
page and line. Written for MySQL 5.5, safe to run twice. With several companies,
run it on every company database (Super Admin: Companies > Database updates >
Update all companies). Until it is run, the program shows a page naming it.

database/accounting_system.sql and accounting_system_clean.sql (new installs only)
include the change. Do NOT import those two files over a live database.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the new
assets/js and assets/css files.

Set-up after the upgrade
------------------------
1. Cooperative > Passbook Types: open each passbook type.
   - Check its Kind (Savings passbook or Loan passbook).
   - Passbook Type Setup, in tups (1 inch = 1,440 tups), as in the former program:
     Cover Top / Left, Header Top / Left, Footer Top / Left, Passbook Body Top /
     Left and Interval, Start Line Skip, No. of Line Skip, Max Lines.
     Former program, Regular Passbook: 900/2400, 700/300, 8000/100, 1230/200,
     275, 12, 1, 26. Loan Passbook: the same with Body Left 1, Start Line Skip 13,
     No. of Line Skip 2, Max Lines 29.
   - The passbook size (page width and height), the font, the characters per
     inch (20 is the condensed print of the former passbooks) and the font size.
   - The design: on the Cover, Header, Body and Footer tabs, place each field
     (drag it on the drawing, or type Left / Top / Width in tups), its alignment
     and its format. "Use the default ..." puts back the former program's layout.
   - Save, then "Print a test page" and adjust until every field falls on its
     place in the passbook.
   A type not saved since version 33 keeps printing with its settings of before
   (shown in the design) until it is saved here.
2. Savings & Deposit Products and Loan Products: a savings product now lists only
   the savings passbooks, a loan product only the loan passbooks. Check the
   passbook type of each product.
3. Accounts printed before: the next line of a passbook is where it was. A new
   passbook (Passbook Printing > New passbook) starts on page 1, line 1.

What changed (see docs/ADDITIONAL_MODULE_V6_CHECKLIST.md)
--------------------------------------------------------
- Capacity to Pay: the entries saved on a loan application (monthly income,
  spouse income, net disposable income, dependents, employer, business, spouse)
  are where the borrower's next application starts. When a new application is
  opened (or the member is chosen on it), they are filled from the member's last
  application, as last saved; a note says which one. What is updated replaces
  them, the rest stays. A first loan starts from the Financial Information tab.
  A renewal or restructuring starts the same way. The application shows a
  Capacity to pay card.
- Credit Investigation: a new investigation starts from the borrower's last one:
  the spouse, addresses, residence, areas and values, informants, children,
  sources of income, assets and character references, with its findings shown
  for reference. Once saved, an investigation shows its own entries. A row is
  saved when any of its boxes is filled (an income with its employer and amount
  but no source name was left out before). The Sources of income list is now
  across the page, so its boxes are wide enough; on a phone every row is a card.
- Passbook printing follows the former program: printing starts on line 1 (not
  line 13); Start Line Skip and No. of Line Skip leave the centre fold blank (with
  13 and 2, after line 12 the next entry prints on line 15); after Max Lines the
  next entry goes to line 1 of the next page, after the header. The line number
  prints beside the date.
- The loan passbook has its own design, separate from the savings passbook:
  the header prints the Loan Type, the Term (e.g. 36mos) and the Due Date; each
  line the line no., Date, Reference No., Release, Loan Payment, Loan Balance,
  Interest Payment, Fines / Surcharges Payment and Teller ID. The release is on
  line 1. The loan's charges (penalties, adjustments, waivers) are not passbook
  lines.
- Passbook Types is the Passbook Type Setup of the former program, with a design
  facility for the cover, header, body and footer (in place of the Crystal Report
  templates): the fields, their place, width, alignment, format and size, any
  passbook size, condensed print, a live drawing with sample data, and test
  pages (a page with sample entries, the line numbers of a page, the cover).
- Passbook Printing: savings or loan passbook; the header prints first on a new
  page; Print the header of a page alone; reprints of loan passbooks too; a fold
  line cannot be chosen as the next line.
