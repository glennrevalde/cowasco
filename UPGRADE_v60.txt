G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 60 (2026-11-15)
=================================================================

The accounting update: the accounting side of the program brought up to G-SMaRT Accounting
version 40 (its versions 35 to 40), from accounting-system (2).zip. Only the accounting features
are taken. The links of the cooperative members to the documents (invoices, sales receipts, cash
vouchers, check vouchers, journal entries and the others) and to water billing are left exactly as
they are: no member is added to a document, a list or a drop-down by this update. See
docs/ACCOUNTING_UPDATE_V40_CHECKLIST.md.

This package holds only the files changed or added since version 59. Copy the folder over your
installation, keeping the folder structure. config/config.php is not included and is never
changed. Works on MySQL 5.5 and MySQL 8 as before.

What to run on the database (once)
----------------------------------
  database/migrations/2027_05_09_accounting_update_v40.sql

  phpMyAdmin: click the database > Import > choose the file > Go
  or:         mysql -u root -p your_database < database/migrations/2027_05_09_accounting_update_v40.sql

Back up the database first. The script is safe to run more than once. Until it is run the
program stops with "Database update needed" and names this file. On the platform (several
companies), Super Admin > Database updates runs it on every company.

It adds: four columns on items for the FB POS (shown on it, its menu group, its order, its
picture) and the table fb_pos_groups; the table pos_cash_counts (the bills and coins counted when
a shift is closed); the tables txn_attachments and txn_attachment_chunks (the files attached to
transactions, kept in the database in pieces of 256 KB, so a file of any size is saved under
MySQL 5.5's default 1 MB packet limit); and three pages with their page access: Point of Sale >
FB POS (for the roles that open the POS Register), FB POS Menu & Groups (for the roles that open
POS Terminals) and Reports > Cash Count Sheet (for the roles that open the X- and Z-Reading
Summary, and for the cashiers). Nothing is deleted and no amount changes.

After copying the files and running the script:
  1. Press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser (the page script and the style sheet changed).
  2. Run System Administration > Utilities: Verify & Rebuild Data > Rebuild data once, so the
     average costs follow the dates (see "Inventory" below).
  3. The item pictures of the FB POS are saved in storage/items (storage/companies/cNNN/items for
     the other companies): the web server must be able to write in the storage folder, as it
     already must for backups and the company logo.

PHP upload size (recommended)
-----------------------------
A new PHP install takes files of up to 2 MB (upload_max_filesize) and 8 MB in one save
(post_max_size). The Attachments box shows the limit in force. For scanned copies raise them in
php.ini, for example upload_max_filesize = 25M and post_max_size = 30M (XAMPP: xampp\php\php.ini),
and restart Apache. A save carrying more than post_max_size is refused with a message that says so.

What is new (the details are in docs/accounting/ACCOUNTING_UPGRADE_v35.txt ... v40.txt and in
the manual)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Inventory: Disassemble / Process Items and the average cost
- A processing order is not saved when the cost of its outputs differs by more than 2% from the
  cost of its inputs plus labor and other costs; the form shows the difference and its percentage
  under the outputs as you type, with a red warning beyond 2%. Bills, item receipts, checks, cash
  vouchers and the other transactions are never checked against it.
- A back-dated, edited, voided or deleted processing order has the later inventory transactions
  costed again in date order (as Rebuild data does), so the averages and the cost of sales follow
  the dates; a blue message says how many, and the audit trail records it.
- System Administration > Company, Security & Closing Date > Inventory and POS: the difference
  allowed (2%), what starts the costing again (Disassemble / Process Items only, every inventory
  transaction, or never) and the most transactions costed again at once (2,000).
- The water bills and the cooperative documents that move stock are costed again with the others,
  their entries unchanged.
- Inventory Center: one "New item" button with every kind of item.

Vendors and Banking
- Write Checks, Enter Bills, supplier credits, Enter Bill for Received Items: the Customer:Job
  column of the Expenses tab is "Customer / Supplier". A supplier chosen on a line goes with the
  line into the general ledger (its subsidiary ledger, Expenses by Vendor), even when the check is
  made out to someone else; Billable is for customers only. The customers of the list are those of
  Customer:Job as before (a member by its member type); a member's own supplier record is not
  added to the list.

"Add new" in the drop-downs
- The Customer, Vendor, name, Account and Item boxes of the transactions end their list with
  "Add new ..." (Add "Northwind Packaging" as a new supplier, with a name typed). The list's own
  form opens in a window over the transaction with the name filled in; saved, the new record is
  chosen in the box. For the users who may create that kind of record. A cooperative member is
  never added from a drop-down: members are entered on the Member Center as before.

Point of Sale
- FB POS: a second selling screen for a food and beverage counter, the items as pictures under
  menu groups; it sells exactly as the POS Register does (the same terminals, shifts, payment
  methods, price levels, receipts and reports). FB POS Menu & Groups; Item List > the item > FB POS
  (the picture, shown on the FB POS, the menu group and the order).
- Cash Count Sheet: Shifts, Cash Drops & Readings > Close register > "Cash counted in drawer" opens
  the sheet of bills and coins; the quantities are kept with the shift. New report Point of Sale >
  Reports > Cash Count Sheet, printed on a quarter page (1/4 Letter or 1/4 Government Legal) with
  "Remitted by" and "Received by".
- POS Receipts, Returns & Voids: From and To with a time, a Cashier filter, and the totals at the
  foot.
- Gross sales of a shift: an item group's price variance is part of the gross sales.

Financial reports
- General Ledger: a Cashier filter (only the transactions that cashier entered, the beginning
  balance too).
- Every report has Print and Save as PDF, side by side: Save as PDF makes the PDF file of the report
  (title, headings, columns, rows, totals, signature lines; the paper and orientation of Modify
  Report > Page; each page with who printed it, when and the page number) and records it in the
  audit trail.

Attachments
- Make Journal Entries, Write Checks, Cash Voucher, Enter Bills (also Receive Items and Enter Bills,
  and Enter Bill for Received Items), Create Purchase Orders, Create Sales Orders and Create Invoices
  have an Attachments box: add files (or drop them on the box), saved with the transaction; the page
  of a saved one lists them with View, Download and Remove, and Add files attaches more later. The
  lists show a paper clip with the number of files. Program files (.exe, .bat ...) are refused. The
  files go with every backup (written as hex, under 1 MB a statement). A change to a saved journal
  entry that waits for approval attaches its files at once (they change no amount).
