G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 53 (2026-11-07)
=================================================================

The accounting update: the accounting side of the program brought up to G-SMaRT Accounting
version 34 (its versions 26 to 34). The cooperative and water features that work through the
accounting (members, their price levels, savings, loans, teller, share capital, water bills
and collections) keep working as before.

This package holds only the files changed or added since version 52. Copy the folder over
your installation, keeping the folder structure. config/config.php is not included and is
never changed. Works on MySQL 5.5 and MySQL 8 as before.

What to run on the database (once)
----------------------------------
  database/migrations/2027_04_04_accounting_update.sql

  phpMyAdmin: click the database > Import > choose the file > Go
  or:         mysql -u root -p your_database < database/migrations/2027_04_04_accounting_update.sql

Back up the database first. The script is safe to run more than once. Until it is run the
program stops with "Database update needed" and names this file. On the platform (several
companies), Super Admin > Database updates runs it on every company.

It adds: the item group columns and the table item_group_sales, the account 4070 Item Group
Price Variance (or the first free of 4071, 4075, 4080, 4090) as the module default account,
transactions.received_via, the table clearing_settlements, and fourteen report pages with
their page access (each given to the roles that open the report beside it). It also sets the
quantity on hand of every item at every site to the total of its movements and the average
cost to the value left over the quantity left (nothing is deleted, no posted amount changes).

After copying the files and running the script, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser, then run
System Administration > Utilities: Verify & Rebuild Data > Verify data. If it lists items
"costed out of order", run Rebuild data (costs).

What is new (the details are in docs/accounting/ACCOUNTING_UPGRADE_v28.txt ... v34.txt and in
the manual)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Point of Sale and inventory reports
- Ending Inventory - POS Cashier (Point of Sale > Reports): what the outlet holds at the end
  of a cashier's shift, by item code or by description, printed on the roll printer; a
  Shift box fills in the dates and times. Shifts, Cash Drops & Readings links to it.
- A time after each date on the inventory movement reports (shifts of a 24-hour business),
  a Cashier filter, "Exclude zero sales", "Exclude zero qty" on Quantity on Hand by Site and
  on the Physical Inventory Worksheet, and totals on POS Sales by Hour.
Item groups
- An item group has a Group price, its Consolidated price (its items at their own prices)
  and the Price variance with its GL account. Sold as one line on estimates, sales orders,
  invoices, sales receipts, statement charges, credit memos and at the POS; saved as its
  items plus one price variance line; shown "by item group" or "item details".
- A member pays the group at the member's price level, as for any item (the member's own
  level, else the members' level of the line of business).
- Sales by Item Group and POS Sales by Item Group. System Administration > Company, Security
  & Closing Date: Track Item Group Sales transactions, Adopt the Group Price in the Item Group.
Sales with no cost
- An inventory part (or an item of a group) with no unit cost and no average cost is not
  sold from a sales form or the POS ("Block sales of items with no unit cost", on by default),
  and a processing order is not posted with an input or output at no cost. Water bills and
  the other documents the water module posts from its own records are not checked.
Purchases
- Receive Items List and Receive Items and Enter Bills List; the Receipt List button.
Banking
- Make Deposits: the card and e-wallet balances are their General Ledger balances on the
  deposit date, with a Details window (what the balance is made of, the receipts not yet
  settled). Tick Settle on the receipts the provider paid: their total goes into Gross
  settled and they leave the unsettled list when the deposit is saved.
- Journal entries to Undeposited Funds are listed on Make Deposits (a debit adds, a credit
  deducts).
- A payment on account taken at the POS with a card or e-wallet goes to its clearing account.
Financial reports
- Comparative Income Statement and Balance Sheet by Month, Quarter, Year and Custom Range /
  Date; Trial Balance by Class; Statement of Cash Flows by Class (the Class List has buttons
  for both). The Statement of Cash Flows by Class follows the account types (Financial >
  Account Types) as the plain statement does; its Total column is the plain statement, the
  Trial Balance by Class's Total columns the Trial Balance, both with the balances between
  the lines of business eliminated.
- Sort by on the sales reports; the Report Center with a search box and a Financial Reports
  card.
Inventory costing
- On one date the stock received is counted before the stock used or sold. The Inventory
  Center history is in that order, with dates, Latest 100 / 500 / All and the balance brought
  forward. A receipt into stock sold below zero settles the shortfall at the cost received
  (the difference to cost of goods sold). New report Average Cost Changes.
