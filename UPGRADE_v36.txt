G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 36 (2027-01-31)
=================================================================

The requests of ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_WATER_COOP_SYSTEMS_v7.xlsx. This package holds
only the files added or changed since version 35. Copy the folder over your
installation, keeping the folder structure. config/config.php is not included and
is never changed.

Database
--------
Run ONE new migration on your database (phpMyAdmin: select the database > Import;
Navicat: Execute SQL File; or php tools/upgrade.php), after backing it up:

    database/migrations/2027_01_24_account_types.sql

It:
- adds the table account_types: the account types of Chart of Accounts > Account
  Types. The fifteen built-in types (Bank, Accounts Receivable, ... Other Expense)
  are written as its first rows, each linked to its section of the Statement of
  Cash Flows (Bank: cash and cash equivalents; Accounts Receivable, Other Current
  Asset and the current liabilities: operating; Fixed Asset and Other Asset:
  investing; Long Term Liability and Equity: financing);
- adds accounts.type_id: the type chosen for an account (empty = its built-in
  type, which every account keeps until you give it another);
- adds the pages Chart of Accounts > Account Types (with the rights of Chart of
  Accounts), and the reports Balance Sheet Summary and Income Statement Summary
  (with the rights of the Balance Sheet and of the Income Statement).

Nothing is deleted, no amount changes and every account stays of the type it is.
Written for MySQL 5.5, safe to run twice. With several companies, run it on every
company database (Super Admin: Companies > Database updates > Update all
companies). Until it is run, the program shows a page naming it.

database/accounting_system.sql and accounting_system_clean.sql (new installs only)
include the change; the sample file also has the two example types below. Do NOT
import those two files over a live database.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the new
assets/css file.

What changed (see docs/ADDITIONAL_MODULE_V7_CHECKLIST.md)
---------------------------------------------------------
- Chart of Accounts > Account Types (new): add account types of your own, such
  as Cash or Loans Receivable. Each one "behaves as" one of the built-in types
  (a Cash type that behaves as Bank is offered wherever money is paid or
  received, exactly like a Bank account), and is linked to a section of the
  Statement of Cash Flows: cash and cash equivalents, operating, investing or
  financing activities. Open a type to give it accounts (ticking a main account
  ticks its sub-accounts) or to move its accounts to another type. The account
  form offers every type, with a note saying what it does and where its accounts
  are shown; a new sub-account takes the type of its main account.
- Statement of Cash Flows: the cash is the accounts of the types linked to cash
  and cash equivalents (named in the note above the report); every other balance
  sheet account is shown once, in the section of its type, under the type's
  heading with "Net change in" the type. The cash at the end of the period equals
  the cash accounts of the Balance Sheet; if not, the note says so in red.
- Balance Sheet and Income Statement (and by Class, by Date Range): every account
  type is a classification of its own: a heading, its accounts and "Total" the
  type. A sub-account is shown under its main account, in the classification of
  the main account's type; a main account with sub-accounts shows them and
  "Total" the main account.
- Chart of Accounts: a main account shows the total of its sub-accounts (its own
  balance, if any, under it). The list is in tree order: each main account
  followed by its sub-accounts. The Type column shows your types, with the
  built-in type each behaves as. Export adds the columns Behaves as and Total
  with sub-accounts.
- New reports under Chart of Accounts > Reports: Balance Sheet Summary and Income
  Statement Summary. They show the main accounts only, each with the total of its
  sub-accounts; every total is that of the Balance Sheet and the Income
  Statement.
- Import Data (accounts): the account type column accepts your own types too.

Set-up after the upgrade
------------------------
Nothing is required: every account keeps its built-in type and the statements
balance as before. To get the classifications of the workbook:

1. Chart of Accounts > Account Types > New account type: Name "Cash", Behaves as
   Bank, On the Statement of Cash Flows: Cash and cash equivalents, Order 5.
   Save. Under "Give accounts this type" tick 1000 Cash (its sub-accounts are
   ticked with it) and any other cash account, then "Give them the type Cash".
2. New account type: Name "Loans Receivable", Behaves as Other Current Asset,
   Operating activities, Order 5. Save, then give it the loans receivable
   accounts and their allowance for probable losses.
3. Open the Balance Sheet, the Balance Sheet Summary and the Statement of Cash
   Flows: Cash and Loans Receivable are classifications of their own, and the
   cash at the end of the cash flow statement equals Total Cash.

Accounts the modules post to (with the word "system" on the Chart of Accounts)
keep their built-in type: they can take a type of yours only when it behaves as
the same built-in type.
