G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 43 (2026-11-01)
=================================================================

The requests of ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_WATER_COOP_SYSTEMS_v14.xlsx. This package holds only
the files added or changed since version 42. Copy the folder over your installation,
keeping the folder structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
One migration, for MySQL 5.5 (back up the database first). It needs the migration of
version 42 (2027_03_14_share_capital_billing.sql) first:

  database/migrations/2027_03_21_share_capital_terms_batch.sql

  phpMyAdmin: click the database > Import > choose the file > Go
  or:         php tools/upgrade.php

It adds four columns and one index:

  share_capital_plans.term_days   the term of a member's fixed billing, in days (empty = no
                                  due date, open)
  share_capital_plans.batch_no    the batch or import that put the member on fixed billing
  share_capital_bills.due_date    the due date of a bill (empty = open)
  share_capital_bills.term_days   the term it was posted with

Every billing and bill on file stays open (no due date), as before. Nothing is deleted
and no amount changes. Safe to run a second time. Until it is run, the program stops
after log-in with a page that names it.

database/accounting_system.sql and accounting_system_clean.sql are written again with
it (a new installation needs nothing else).

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser.

What changed (see docs/ADDITIONAL_MODULE_V14_CHECKLIST.md)
----------------------------------------------------------
- Fixed Share Capital Billing, Add member / Edit: a Term (days) field. Filled in, it
  sets the due date of each bill (its posting date plus the term); blank, the bill has
  no due date (open). The list shows the term of each member (Term 10 days, or No due
  date) and Bills shows the due date of each bill.
- Fixed Share Capital Billing > Batch billing (new): Batch Share Capital Billing.
  Amount, Frequency, Start date, Posting date (day) and Term (days) once, then tick the
  members, each with the share capital account beside the name (Find, tick all shown,
  "With a water-service account only"). Save puts every member ticked on fixed billing,
  or none; the members of one batch share a batch number (SCB-...).
- Fixed Share Capital Billing > Import (new): the members and their share capital
  accounts from a CSV or Excel (.xlsx) file (Excel and CSV templates on the page), with
  values for the blank cells (amount, frequency, start date, posting day, term). Every
  line is shown for review first: a line with a problem in red, with the problem and
  the cell at fault; correct a cell by typing in it, remove a member with x, or remove
  every red line at once. Post billing puts every member on fixed billing, or none (batch
  SCI-...).
- One-Time Billing > Import Charges (new; also linked from Batch Issue Charges): many
  members' charges from a CSV or Excel file, each line with its own charge category
  (code name of Bill Codes), amount (blank: the bill code's price; a material: its
  price times the quantity) and term. The same review (red lines to correct or remove),
  then Issue bills makes one one-time bill and one invoice for each line, as Issue
  Individual Charge does (the member discount, the stock of a material, the class),
  every line or none. The water bill's own bill code is refused.
- Collections (Cashier): a share capital bill shows its due date, or No due date; one
  past its due date is marked Late. No penalty or compromise is suggested on them.
- Excel (.xlsx) files are read when PHP's zip extension is on (in C:\xampp\php\php.ini
  remove the ";" before extension=zip and restart Apache); otherwise choose CSV files.
- The rights: the import pages follow their page (Fixed Share Capital Billing, One-Time
  Billing); posting or issuing takes the Create right. Nothing to set.
