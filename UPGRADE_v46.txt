G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 46 (2026-11-02)
=================================================================

The requests of ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_WATER_COOP_SYSTEMS_v16.xlsx. This package holds only
the files added or changed since version 45. Copy the folder over your installation,
keeping the folder structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
One migration, for MySQL 5.5 (back up the database first). It needs the migrations of
the earlier versions first (the last one: 2027_03_21_share_capital_terms_batch.sql):

  database/migrations/2027_03_28_regular_joint_subgroups_receipts.sql

  phpMyAdmin: click the database > Import > choose the file > Go
  or:         php tools/upgrade.php

It adds, and changes nothing else:

  member_types       the member type "Regular - Joint" (code RGJ) for joint accounts, a
                     regular member, beside Associate - Joint (not added again when a type
                     of that code or name is on file)
  pages              Water Billing > Service & Materials > Address Sub-groups, given to the
                     roles that have Water Service Accounts, with the same rights;
                     Water Billing > Setup > Receipt Layout Setup, given to the roles that
                     have Water Settings, with the same rights

No column or table is added. Nothing is deleted and no amount changes. Safe to run a
second time. Until it is run, the program stops after log-in with a page that names it.

database/accounting_system.sql and accounting_system_clean.sql are written again with
it (a new installation needs nothing else).

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser.

What changed (see docs/ADDITIONAL_MODULE_V16_CHECKLIST.md)
----------------------------------------------------------
- Member Information, Kind of member Joint account: the Type list has "Regular - Joint"
  beside "Associate - Joint". It is the same Joint/Institution Details and Membership
  Information form; the member is a regular member (Member Types, Clusters & Other Lists
  shows it and its minimum share capital).

- Address sub-groups can be added, renamed, sorted, switched off and deleted where they
  are chosen: the "Add, edit or delete sub-groups" link under the Address sub-group box
  of the member form (Water Defaults and Loan and savings grouping) and of Water Service
  Accounts (the filter, Set for Selected, Add account) opens the list in a window; the
  boxes on the page take the change at once. Water Billing > Service & Materials >
  Address Sub-groups has the same list on a page. A new name is carried to every member
  and water-service account that has the old one; a sub-group in use cannot be deleted
  (switch it off so it is no longer offered). Every change is in the audit trail.

- Reading Entry > Recorded Readings: Edit and Delete on each unposted reading. Edit opens
  the reading with its previous and present reading, recomputes the consumption and the
  amount as you type, and saves it the way the entry form does. Delete asks for a reason
  (kept in the audit log) and the account can then be read again for the period. A posted
  (billed) or paid reading shows "Posted, locked" and is corrected through Billing
  Adjustments, as before; a reading followed by a reading of a later period also stays.
  Edit needs the Create or Edit right on Reading Entry; Delete needs that and the Delete
  right.

- Incremental searching on Water Billing. Every list box with a search on the Water
  Billing pages (the Consumer Account of Installment, One-Time and Recurring Billing, the
  accounts, the bill codes and every other long list) narrows as you type: one letter is
  enough, the names that
  start with what is typed come first, the letters typed are marked, Up and Down move,
  Enter takes the highlighted one (Tab takes a match only when one is left), Escape
  closes; typing on the closed box opens it with the letters. The member searches of Reading Entry, Generate SOA / Post Billing and
  Collections (Cashier) search from the first letter and are moved with the arrow keys.
  The searches of Water Service Accounts, Billing List, Billing Adjustments, Disconnection
  Management, Collection Corrections and Discounted Members show their results as you type,
  without pressing Search; Payment History and the Installment ledger narrow the table as
  you type; Customer Ledger finds an account by any words of its name.

- Time deposits: the accrued interest a member withdrew before maturity (Withdraw accrued
  interest, CAIW) is now deducted from the accrued interest. The Subsidiary vs General
  Ledger Reconciliation compared the accrued interest of the deposits, without the
  withdrawals, with account 2320, which the withdrawals had debited: the 500.00 of
  Bautista, Pedro showed as a difference. The subsidiary ledger is now the accrued interest
  less what was withdrawn of it, for each accrued-interest account of the products, and
  the member list behind it agrees. The time deposit's Interest accruals lists each
  accrual and each withdrawal with the balance (997.26 accrued, 500.00 withdrawn: 497.26),
  and the Time Deposit Register and the Accrued Interest report show the payable.
  Nothing in the books changes: the journal entries were right.

- Printed or Pre-Printed receipts. Company, Security & Closing Date > Water Billing:
  "Collection: print receipt type" and "Official receipt type", each Printed (the whole
  receipt on plain paper, as before) or Pre-Printed (only the entries, on the
  cooperative's own printed forms). Water Billing > Setup > Receipt Layout Setup sets up
  each form as a passbook type is set up: the paper (water bill 22 x 10 cm with two
  copies, the official receipt 9 x 5.5 in, or your own), the top, bottom, left and right
  margins, the font and its size and pitch, and for the header, the lines and the totals
  where each entry prints (Left, Top, Width, alignment, format, size), dragged on the
  drawing of the form or typed in the table. Print a test page prints the saved layout
  with sample entries. The water bill / collection receipt and the official receipt then
  print only their entries on the form; a link on each switches to the other kind for one
  print. The default layouts were measured from the printed receipts of the program.

- Generate Official Receipt: the values panel no longer cuts off the amount boxes and the
  buttons; the pre-printed form is shown whole on a phone.
