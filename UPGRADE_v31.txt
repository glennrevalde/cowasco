G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 31 (2026-12-27)
=================================================================

The requests of ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_WATER_COOP_SYSTEMS_v3.xlsx. This package holds
only the files added or changed since version 30. Copy the folder over your
installation, keeping the folder structure. config/config.php is not included and
is never changed.

Database
--------
Run ONE new migration on your database (phpMyAdmin: select the database > Import;
Navicat: Execute SQL File; or php tools/upgrade.php), after backing it up:

    database/migrations/2026_12_27_member_defaults_savings_center.sql

It adds:
- the member's Water Defaults on the members table (zone, sequence, rate code,
  pipe size, connection date, disconnection date, connection status and the
  member's own water-service account) and water_accounts.connection_date;
- on sav_txns: trans_no (the Savings Transaction Center number), the opposite
  account, the cheque details, back entry, and the error-correction links;
- sav_txn_codes.is_memo, roles.coop_cash_account_id (the profile cash account),
  the passbook layout columns of passbook_types (header_json, cover_json,
  skip_lines, dash_empty, footer_text), td_placements.accrued_paid and the table
  td_interest_payouts;
- the number series sav_trans; the error-correct codes ECSD, ECSW, ECM, EDM and one
  ECK.. for each check deposit code; CAIW (cash accrued interest withdrawal) and
  TDI (time deposit interest credited). CM and DM become memo codes;
- the page Savings & Deposits > Savings Transaction Center, given to the roles
  that open Savings Deposit / Withdrawal, which is now called Savings Account
  Transaction; Time Deposits is now called Time Deposit Center.

Nothing is deleted, no posted amount changes. Written for MySQL 5.5, safe to run
twice. With several companies, run it on every company database (Super Admin:
Companies > Database updates > Update all companies). Until it is run, the program
shows a page naming it.

database/accounting_system.sql and accounting_system_clean.sql (new installs only)
include the change. Do NOT import those two files over a live database.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the new
assets/js and assets/css files.

Set-up after the upgrade
------------------------
1. Cash account of the tellers. A teller transacts with the drawer of the open
   shift, else the cash account on System Administration > User Accounts, else the
   Cash account of the user's role (System Administration > User Role
   Configuration, new field). Set the role's cash account for tellers who have
   none of their own.
2. Savings & Deposits > Savings Transaction Codes. Check the codes: the Opposite
   Account (GL Account, or User Account = the teller's cash), the Savings Entry
   Side, Check Deposit with its Clearing Days, Is Error Correct with its Related
   Trans Code. Add the codes of your former program (for example UBDD - Unionbank
   Direct Deposit: GL Account = your Unionbank account, Credit; CHK3 if you keep
   that mnemonic instead of CK3) and an error-correct code for each.
   "Account closing codes" (same page) sets the codes Close Account posts:
   CSW for the closing withdrawal and CF for the closing fee.
3. Cooperative > Passbook Types, for your savings passbook:
   - Column positions: Line no. (new), Date, Trans code, Deposits, Withdrawals,
     Balance, Teller ID;
   - Every page: the Top and Left of the Account Name and the Account Number;
   - Inside cover: the Top and Left of Account Name, Account Number, Address,
     Contact Number and Branch;
   - Lines never printed on (the centre fold), for example 13;
   - Footer text, and "A dash in the empty amount column";
   - at the bottom, Deposit / withdrawal slip validation: where the machine
     validation prints on the slip.
   Print a test page on Passbook Printing and adjust until the lines fall on the
   passbook rows.
4. Members: the Water Defaults, Customer Defaults and Vendor Defaults tabs of
   Member Information hold what Water Billing, the Customer Center and the Vendor
   Center used separate forms for. A member's customer and supplier records are
   made by the program (for members saved before the upgrade, when a customer or
   vendor list is first opened).

What changed (see docs/ADDITIONAL_MODULE_V3_CHECKLIST.md)
--------------------------------------------------------
- Water Billing > Bill Codes: the edit dialog is fully visible and shows the saved
  accounts; every water dialog opens above the side menu.
- General Ledger: the column is "Balance", on the account's normal side (Water
  Sales shows a positive credit balance; a credit increases it).
- Member Information: Water Defaults, Customer Defaults and Vendor Defaults tabs;
  one name per member in every line of business (no member-customer, member-
  vendor or water-consumer form); Find lists the member once.
- Savings Account Center in the former program's layout: toolbar New, Edit,
  Close, Restrict, Notes, Filter, Refresh; search as you type; right-click menu
  Approve, Re-Open, Reset Passbook Print Line, Print Passbook Cover, Reprint
  Passbook, Print Passbook Footer, Update Passbook, Close Account, Delete Account.
  Closing an account posts its closing transaction.
- Savings Transaction Center: every teller savings and time deposit transaction
  in one number series; toolbar Savings, Time Deposit, Memo, Refund, Interest,
  Dividend, R Check, View, Ledger, Filter, Print; Show Transactions Today/Custom.
- Savings Account Transaction: the transaction code decides the journal entry;
  Error Correct; cheque details; Print to Passbook; Show Preview (machine
  validation on the slip); Back Entry; Update Passbook; Reprint Validation;
  Delete; Clear; Save.
- Time Deposit Center: Time Deposit, Rollover, Close, Withdraw Accrued (CAIW),
  Ledger, Filter, Print; the accrued interest can be withdrawn during the term.
- Passbook printing: line numbers, the account name and number on every page, the
  inside cover, the fold left blank, error corrections printed negative, the
  teller ID; reset of the print line.
- Official Receipt: savings, share capital and loan payments are no longer taken
  on it (Savings Account Transaction and Loan Collection take them).
