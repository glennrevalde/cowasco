G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 50 (2026-11-07)
=================================================================

This package holds only the files changed since version 49. Copy the folder over your
installation, keeping the folder structure. config/config.php is not included and is
never changed. No migration: nothing to run on the database. The only program file is
water/common/setupReadinessHelpers.php; the rest are tests and notes.

What changed
------------
- An open billing period is no longer a step of the water set-up guide. "Billing
  readiness" ("No open billing period exists for new readings") blocked Reading Entry,
  Post Billing and the other billing pages until a period was open. The guide now has 6
  required steps and disappears once they are done. Without an open period Reading
  Entry says so itself ("No open reading periods found. Please create or open a reading
  period first.") and its Continue button stays off; saving a reading and posting bills
  still refuse a period that is not open, as before. A period is created on Water
  Billing > Billing > Bill Period Management.
