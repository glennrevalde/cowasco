G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 58 (2026-11-14)
=================================================================

The requests of ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_WATER_COOP_SYSTEMS_v23.xlsx (docs/ADDITIONAL_MODULE_V23_CHECKLIST.md).

This package holds only the files changed or added since version 57. Copy the folder over your
installation, keeping the folder structure. config/config.php is not included and is never
changed. Works on MySQL 5.5 and MySQL 8 as before.

Version 57 must be in place first (its files and database/migrations/2027_05_02_bill_accounts_center.sql).

What to run on the database
---------------------------
Nothing. Version 58 adds no table, column or page. Press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser afterwards
(the script and the style sheet of Consolidated Import changed).

What is new
-----------
Members & Others > Consolidated Import: the cut-off date of each row
- A "Cut-Off Date" column in the file (also read as cut_off_date, Cutoff Date, As of Date) dates
  the transactions the row brings: the savings balances, the totals paid on the loans, the time
  deposits' principal and accrued interest, and the bill balances.
- On the member's row it is the member's cut-off date: it dates every line of the member that has
  no date of its own, also the accounts on the rows under the member. On a row under the member it
  dates the lines of that row only.
- A line's own date column comes first (savings_cut_off_date, loan_cut_off_date,
  td_cut_off_date, bill_date), the Cut-off date of the page last.
- The member line shows its cut-off date in the review (corrected there like any cell); each good
  line says the date it is entered on ("as of 10/31/2026"). A cut-off date that is not a date, or is
  after today, is in red.
- The vouchers are one for each cut-off date and code, as before.

Members & Others > Consolidated Import: time deposits
- The time deposits of the members in the same file, any number a member, on the member's row or
  on a row under it: td_principal, td_accrued_interest, td_date_deposited, td_term, td_rate and
  td_maturity_date (headings such as "TD Principal", "Date Deposit", "TD Interest Rate", "Maturity
  Date" are recognised), and if you have them td_product, td_certificate_no, td_maturity_action,
  td_cut_off_date and td_txn_code. A second one on the same row: td2_principal ...
- The term or the maturity date is enough: the other is worked out from the date deposited. The
  rate, when blank, is the product's rate for the principal and term; the product, when blank, is
  the time deposit product when there is only one (or write its code or its name).
- Each time deposit is a line of its own tab, checked as Import Time Deposit checks it (the product,
  the minimum and maximum placement, the dates, the rate, the certificate no.), in red with its
  reason and cell when it has a problem, corrected in place or removed.
- Saved Active with its certificate, rate, term and maturity date; its principal (credited to Time
  Deposits) and its accrued interest (credited to the accrued interest payable) entered on its
  cut-off date with the savings transaction code. Saving them needs the Approve right on the Time
  Deposit Center.
- The templates (Excel and CSV, both layouts) have the Cut-Off Date in the last column and a time
  deposit; the Columns sheet of the Excel template explains every column.
- Import time deposits (Time Deposit Center) has a Consolidated Import button.
