G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 57 (2026-11-08)
=================================================================

The requests of ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_WATER_COOP_SYSTEMS_v22.xlsx (docs/ADDITIONAL_MODULE_V22_CHECKLIST.md).

This package holds only the files changed or added since version 56. Copy the folder over your
installation, keeping the folder structure. config/config.php is not included and is never
changed. Works on MySQL 5.5 and MySQL 8 as before.

Version 56 must be in place first (its files and database/migrations/2027_04_25_consolidated_import.sql).

What to run on the database (once)
----------------------------------
  database/migrations/2027_05_02_bill_accounts_center.sql

  phpMyAdmin: click the database > Import > choose the file > Go
  or:         mysql -u root -p your_database < database/migrations/2027_05_02_bill_accounts_center.sql

It adds the page Water Billing > Billing > Bill Accounts Center and gives it to the roles that have
Billing List, Generate SOA / Post Billing or Collections (Cashier). No table is added or changed.
Safe to run more than once. Until it is run the program stops with "Database update needed" and
names this file. Press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser afterwards (the receipt's style sheet changed).

What is new
-----------
Water Billing > Billing > Bill Accounts Center (after Billing Management)
- Every bill of the members and customers in one grid, laid out like the Loan Accounts Center: the
  water bills, the one-time, recurring and installment bills (each amortization) and the fixed
  share capital bills, one line per invoice. It opens on the open bills (not fully paid); the
  closed (fully paid) bills, the overdue ones, the ones not yet due, or all of them, are a filter
  away.
- The columns: Account No., Name, Description, Bill Date, Invoice No., Due Date, Amount, Paid,
  Balance, Status. Under the grid the count, the overdue bills and the totals of every bill found.
- Filters: bill status, bill code, zone, address sub-group, members or customers, bill type, bill
  dates, and the order. Search as you type by name, account no., invoice no. or member no.
- Export to Excel (.xlsx) or CSV, Print, or Save as PDF from the print window: every bill found.
- From a bill: Collection (the cashier with the account chosen), Statement (the Individual
  Statement of Account), Ledger and Account, on the toolbar, by double-click or right-click.

Financial > Chart of Accounts > Import
- The accounts of a chart of accounts from a CSV file or an Excel workbook: code, name and type,
  and if you have them the main account, normal balance, With SL, Active, description and bank
  account no. The columns in any order and in their usual names; the chart's own Export is read
  back as it is.
- Every line is shown in a review form before anything is saved: a line with a problem is in red
  with its reason and its cell, any cell can be corrected (checked again at once) and any line
  removed. Save saves every account or none, the main accounts first.
- An account whose code is already on the chart is updated from its line (blank cells keep what is
  saved) or left as it is, as you choose. The rules of the account form apply.

Member Information > Water Defaults (and Loan and savings grouping)
- The address sub-group is edited right in its box: the pencil renames the sub-group chosen (every
  member and water-service account with it takes the new name), + adds a new one and chooses it.
  "All sub-groups" under the box still opens the whole list (order, switch off, delete).

The water bill / collection receipt (Collections, Payment History)
- Printed on Letter bond paper, portrait: the Cooperative's copy on the upper half, the
  Consumer's copy on the lower half, cut apart on the dashed line.
- Black text only; the entries in bold Courier New and the labels in bold Arial, at sizes an Epson
  dot-matrix printer prints clearly.
- No header or footer of the browser any more (no date, title, web address or page number): the
  page has no browser margins.
- The pre-printed receipt (Receipt Layout Setup) is the same Letter sheet by default, in the new
  "Dot matrix, bold" font. A layout you saved without changing the former default is read as the
  new default; a layout you changed stays as it is, with a note that "Back to the default layout"
  prints on Letter paper.
- In the print window choose Letter paper and Actual size / 100%.
