## 2. Decisions

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | The Loan Due figures are the Loan Collection form's (`LoanTxn::dueDetails`) as of the Date of Transaction. | The cashier then tells the member the same amounts the teller will collect; the penalty includes the penalty still to charge, as on the form. |
| D2 | The water account's member is the member of the same name (party); every branch's loans are counted. | That is how the cashier page already finds the member's share capital bills; a member's loans are owed whatever branch released them. |
| D3 | "Penalties" (plural, as drawn) and a breakdown on the Total as well. | The mock-up says Penalties; the Total's breakdown costs nothing and answers the next question. |
| D4 | Member type is shown wherever Customer type is. | Both narrow the same customers (`Report::customerIds`); a member type filter only on one report would leave the other sales reports unable to separate the members' sales. |
| D5 | Non-members include the walk-in sales. | A sale with no customer is not a member's sale. |
| D6 | In the new report a member's customer goes under its member type even when it has a customer type. | The report is about the members' sales by member type; the others are grouped by customer type. |
