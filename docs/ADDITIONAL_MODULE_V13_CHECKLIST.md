## 2. Decisions

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | The transaction code and the class of a collection are set once on the page, not per member. | V62-AA70 has one "TRANSACTION CODE UPON COLLECTION" and one "CLASS" above the list. |
| D2 | The cashier collects the bills on the member's water-service account (the same party). | M56: "part of the collection of the cashier in the water billing"; the cashier works by water-service account, and a member and a consumer are one person through the Party ID. A member without one is flagged on the page. |
| D3 | A bill is posted on its posting day, lazily (page, cashier) and by an optional daily task; the past days of a new billing are posted at once. | "upon reaching the day the system should post the amount for collection"; the program has no daemon, and a billing started earlier is expected to have those contributions. |
| D4 | No approval and no teller shift for these deposits; the product's minimum deposit does not apply. | They are collected by the water cashier on the water receipt; the amount is set by the billing (the workbook's 50 is under the sample product's 100 minimum). |
| D5 | An edit starts after the month of the last bill; hold skips the days on hold; delete cancels the unpaid bills. | A month must never be billed twice, and a stop must not leave a backlog on resuming. |
| D6 | The code name of a collection is FIXED SHARE CAPITAL. | A bill code named SHARE CAPITAL may exist (a charge billed with the water bill, posted to its own account): "do not consolidate". |
| D8 | The deposit of a water receipt is corrected only through the receipt. | The receipt, the bill and the deposit must change together; a deposit deleted on its own would leave the receipt counting money that is not in the books. |
| D9 | A share capital bill has no due date at the cashier. | It is a contribution, not a receivable: no penalty, compromise or overdue mark. |
| D7 | Detailed lists the code names collected in the period, ordered as Bill Codes (the water bill first, the fixed share capital last). | A column for each code name collected, and none for one not collected. |
