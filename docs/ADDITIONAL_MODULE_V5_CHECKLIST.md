# Additional module for the Water + Coop systems (v5): requirements checklist

This maps every request of the workbook `ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_WATER_COOP_SYSTEMS_v5.xlsx` (Sheet1, 18 screenshots
with notes, rows 40 to 306) to the code that implements it and the test that exercised it. Status: **Done** means
built, reachable from the menu, and run in the tests or by hand on the sample database.

The database change is one upgrade script, `database/migrations/2027_01_10_teller_menu_loan_release_vouchers.sql`
(MySQL 5.5, safe to run twice). New installations get the same through `database/schema.sql` and `tools/install.php`.

## 2. Decisions

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Loan Collection and the Savings Transaction Center keep their page keys (`loan.collection`, `sav.center`); only their place in the menu changes. Switching off the Loans or the Savings line still closes them. | The rights of every role, the links between pages and the users' bookmarks keep working. |
| D2 | The summary counts a savings deposit or withdrawal made on an official receipt once (from the receipt), and one made on the Savings Transaction Center from the savings ledger; an error correction is a line of its own, negative. A loan released in cash from the teller's drawer is cash paid out of the drawer. | Net cash of the summary equals the teller's drawer. |
| D3 | The Cash/bank Account lists the active Bank-type accounts: one with a bank account no. or "Bank" in its name is a bank account (Cheque), the others are cash accounts (Cash). A chart that does not tell them apart offers every account for both. | The chart of accounts has one type for cash and bank. |
| D4 | The voucher ID of a release is its own series (Document Numbering, "Loan release voucher ID", LR-), printed on the voucher; the cash voucher keeps its CAV number and the check its cheque no. The releases of before keep their loan release voucher (LR- number), are shown in the monitoring list and print in the same format. | The numbers of Banking stay in sequence; posted entries of before are not rewritten. |
| D5 | A loan release voucher is not edited or deleted in Banking; it is voided (with the approval rules of the release), which cancels the release; refused once the loan has a payment. | The loan's balances, schedule and charges are built from the voucher. |
| D6 | A cash release from the teller's own cash account is a cash-out of the teller's open shift (refused without a shift); a release from another cash account (Revolving Fund) does not touch a drawer. | The drawer count stays right. |
| D7 | The additional interest and the penalty of the Internal Ledger are computed to the chosen date and shown in italics; they are not posted by opening the ledger. The penalty is charged when the loan is collected (as before); additional interest is collected by raising the interest with Loans > Loan Adjustments and Override Payment on Loan Collection. | Opening a ledger must not change the books; the loan product decides what is charged. |
| D8 | "Additional interest when past due" of the loan product: by the interest method (the default: diminishing loans on each missed amortization, add-on loans after maturity), on each missed amortization, on the balance after maturity, or none. | The former program's set-up per product, with a default that fits each interest method. |

## 3. Upgrade

Back up the database, copy the files over the installation and run
`database/migrations/2027_01_10_teller_menu_loan_release_vouchers.sql` (phpMyAdmin Import, Navicat Execute SQL File,
or `php tools/upgrade.php`). Then follow `UPGRADE_v33.txt`: the cash and bank accounts, the classes, the voucher ID
series, the product's additional interest and the rights on Loan Release Vouchers.
