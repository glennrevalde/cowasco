# Additional module for the Water + Coop systems (v11): requirements checklist

This maps every request of `ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_WATER_COOP_SYSTEMS_v11.xlsx` (version 40) to the code that implements
it and the test that exercised it. Its only sheet, **Sheet2**, holds two screenshots with notes in column N:

- image 1 (top): the **Loan Accounts Center**, a rectangle drawn in its title bar, note N11-N35 (import loans);
- image 2 (bottom): **Import members** of version 39, arrows from **Download the CSV template** and from the card **The
  columns**, note N65 (a template with every other detail of the member).

With the workbook came one more request in the conversation: "provide a checker on the form for uploading files for
the members, savings, time deposits and loans, if the list has an error, color it red if it has errors, so that it can
be deleted or corrected" (request 3 below).

Status: **Done** means built, reachable from the menu, and run in the tests and in a browser on the sample database.

Version 40 has no migration (`UPGRADE_v40.txt`).

## 2. Decisions

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | The loan is rebuilt from its release, not entered as a balance: its schedule and a ledger with the payments on the due dates. | The workbook gives the release terms and the totals paid, and presumes the payments were made on the due dates; the schedule, the arrears, the penalties to come and the passbook then work as for a loan released here. |
| D2 | The journal voucher carries the principal balances only (Dr loan receivable, Cr the balancing account). | Only the principal is a receivable on the books (the interest is income when collected, as on a loan released here); the balancing account takes the other side, as the workbook asks. |
| D3 | The payments of the former system have no voucher and are marked as already in the passbook. | They were posted in the former system; they must not count as cash collected here, appear on Loan Collection or print again in the passbook. |
| D4 | Past due interest is worked out only for a loan matured by the cut-off date, and not when the product's additional interest is None. | N33: "compute its additional past due interest, if the loan is already matured"; a product that charges none is respected. |
| D5 | The worked-out penalties are less the penalties paid, never below zero; the past due interest is less the interest paid beyond the schedule. | The totals paid are lump sums: what is paid on a penalty or beyond the schedule has already met part of what is worked out. |
| D6 | The past due interest is an amortization of its own, due at the cut-off date. | It is then collected as interest due on the Loan Collection Form, oldest first, like the rest. |
| D7 | After the cut-off date the loan runs as any other: penalties from the day after it, the Internal Ledger's additional interest from it. | Nothing before the cut-off date is counted twice. |
| D8 | One voucher per cut-off date and balancing account. | The ledger gets one readable entry per migration batch, as on the other imports. |
| D9 | A row is checked again as it is corrected by sending the rows to the server, not by checking in the browser. | The checks need the database (members, products, balances); the same code checks, reviews and uploads, so a row shown without red is a row that uploads. |
| D10 | A check may name its cells itself (`_cells`); otherwise the cells are found from the column names in the message. | "Spouse: enter the last name" must outline the spouse's cells, not the member's last name. |
| D11 | The full template is a second template; the first stays as it was. | N65 asks for "another template"; a member file of the required fields alone keeps working. |
| D12 | The schedule of an imported loan is numbered in date order, the past due interest among the amortizations by its due date (the cut-off date). | The collection and the dues take the amortizations by their number: the past due interest is then paid before an amortization not yet due, and the last due date is the latest passed. |
| D13 | An optional Accrued interest column; blank, none. | Whether the former books carried the unpaid interest as income (accrual basis) depends on the cooperative: given, it goes to Loan Interest Receivable and is not accrued again; blank, the first interest accrual books the interest due and unpaid, as for a loan released here. |
| D14 | The balancing account is refused when it is a loans receivable or loan interest receivable account. | The entry would debit and credit the same account and leave the loans out of the general ledger. |
| D15 | The Loan Collections report and Home's "collected today" leave out the payments of the former system; the due-versus-collected report keeps them, as it keeps the history's dues. | A collection is money received here; a comparison of what was due and what was paid over a past period is right only with both sides of the history. |
| D16 | The balancing account is typed in the grid (with the page's choice for the rows that leave it blank), not chosen from a list. | A list of every account in every row made the page of a file of a thousand loans 13 MB. |
| D17 | The check as you type sends one check at a time; a change made meanwhile goes with the next, the pause before it grows with the file, and the check lets go of the session at once. | A check of a long file takes seconds on the server: overlapping checks would queue behind the session and load the server for answers already out of date. |
| D18 | The payments of the former system are marked as already in the loan passbook, the release too. | The member's passbook of the former system already shows them; the next line printed is the first collection here, with the balance. |
