## 2. Decisions

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | The file is read into the database and reviewed there, a page of lines at a time. | Thousands of rows do not fit a form posted back and forth (PHP's `max_input_vars` and `post_max_size`), and the review survives a closed browser: the file is opened again from **Files read**. |
| D2 | One line per member, savings account, loan and bill balance. | A wrong loan is corrected or removed without the member's other accounts; the checks of each import run on its own lines. |
| D3 | The checks and the saving are those of the four imports, called with a member not on file yet. | The same rules, messages and entries as Import Members, Import Savings, Import Loans and Upload Bill Balances; nothing written twice. |
| D4 | A member and all its lines are saved in one transaction, a few members at a time. | A member is never saved without some of its accounts; a problem found while saving stops that group only, and saving goes on after it is corrected. |
| D5 | A member no. already on file adds the file's accounts to that member and leaves its details as they are. | A member's accounts may come in a later file; its details are changed on Member Information. |
| D6 | A fixed share capital balance of a new member needs `fsc_amount` (the fixed billing is set up with the member, from the day after the cut-off date). | As in Upload Bill Balances (v55, D4), a share capital bill belongs to a fixed billing whose amount someone chose. |
