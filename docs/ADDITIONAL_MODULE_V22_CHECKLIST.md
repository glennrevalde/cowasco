## 2. Decisions

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | The Bill Accounts Center lists the bills as the Individual Statement of Account and the cashier know them (the same invoice numbers, amounts and payments). | A bill has one balance everywhere: what the center shows as open is what the statement lists and the cashier collects. |
| D2 | An installment shows each amortization as a bill, dated on its due date. | Each amortization is billed and collected on its own, and has its own due date and status. |
| D3 | A fixed share capital bill is shown on the member's first water-service account, or on its share capital account when the member has no water service. | As the Statement of Account shows it; the zone and address sub-group filters then find it with the member's water service. |
| D4 | The plain-paper receipt prints the two copies one above the other on one Letter sheet. | Two copies of the receipt fit a portrait Letter sheet only stacked; each copy is then large enough for a dot-matrix printer. |
| D5 | The chart of accounts import updates the accounts on the chart by default (blank cells keep what is saved), with the choice to leave them as they are. | As System Administration > Import from CSV does; the chart's own Export is read back with nothing to change. |
