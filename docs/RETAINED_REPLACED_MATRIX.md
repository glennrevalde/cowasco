# Features retained and replaced

What each part of the two supplied systems became in the one integrated system. "Retained" means the page and its
code still run (adapted to the shared login, menu, party master and ledger); "Replaced" means the page of the other
system now does the job and the former page answers with a notice and a link (old bookmarks keep working). Nothing
listed here was dropped without a replacement.

## Cooperative system

Every module of the cooperative system is **retained** unchanged in behaviour: Chart of Accounts, Fixed Assets,
Customers (A/R), Vendors (A/P), Inventory, Point of Sale, Banking, BIR Reports, System Administration, Company
(multi-company), Parties (Party Master, Identity Review, Consolidated Party Statement), Cooperative (members,
applications, share capital, lines of business), Savings & Deposits, Loans, Teller, Year-end Distribution (interest on
share capital, patronage refund) and CDA Compliance. Its test suite (54 cases, 880 checks) passes on both decimal
engines.

What the integration added to it:

| Cooperative component | Addition |
|---|---|
| Party Master / Party Center | A Water tab: the party's water-service accounts with water and ledger balances and links to collect, the water ledger and the account statement. Party search finds water account numbers. |
| Consolidated Party Statement | Water bills, collections and adjustments in their own section; amounts due from the party and share/savings interests stay apart (never netted). |
| Ledger engine (`Txn`) | Documents of water events are changed only by the water module; cooperative payment, credit memo and POS screens do not offer water bills (they are collected at the water cashier). |
| Lines of business | The Water Utility (WU) business unit: every water document and ledger line carries it, so the water statements are separate and nothing is counted twice in the consolidated ones; water is outside the patronage refund basis. |
| Inventory menu | Material Issuance listed under Inventory as well. |
| Module Default Accounts | A Water group: A/R, metered water sales, penalty, compromise, discounts, default collections cash. |
| Backup & Restore | The water compatibility views are re-created on restore. |
| System Updates | `2026_12_01_water_billing.sql`, safe to run again; detected as pending until its tables and views exist. |
