  Net columns at the bottom of the list with a text box "Total", note B36;

- image 3 (A72:M102): the **Patronage Refund** batch `dist.pr&id=2` in review, with arrows to the Basis column, note

## 2. Decisions

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | The window opens on pointing (after a short pause), on Tab and on a click or tap, and shows the computation worked out from the records when it opens, not a copy saved with the batch. | The workbook asks for the details on hover. Working it out again from the records with the same arithmetic shows the actual entries (with links to the documents) and reveals an entry made after the batch was computed; the batch keeps its own figures until it is recomputed. |
| D2 | The window is fetched from the page when first opened and kept, not written into the page. | A batch can have thousands of members, and a member's patronage hundreds of documents; the page stays quick. |
| D3 | The totals are those of the members on the list (what is distributed), with a line for the basis of the members removed and, while the list is narrowed, a line for the members shown. | The members removed have a basis but no amount; adding them in would make the total basis disagree with the tiles and the register. |
| D4 | Delete is for a batch never posted (in review, reviewed or approved); a posted batch is still reversed, and a reversed batch stays on file. An approved batch needs the Approve right as well as Delete. | A posted batch has vouchers and member entries, which are corrected by a reversal, never removed. An approval is the decision of an approver, so only an approver may undo it. |
| D5 | The batch and its lines are removed from the tables; the audit trail keeps them in full with the reason, and the batch number is not used again. | Nothing refers to a batch that was never posted; the record of who deleted what and why stays, and the numbers of the batches stay in sequence without being reused. |
| D6 | No migration. | The Delete right on the two pages already exists and is given to the General Manager and the Branch Manager. |
