  "Enter the loan number in the search box." (an arrow to the box); notes N51 to N53;

## 2. Decisions

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | A loan adjustment is a line of the loan passbook (code AJ), printed at once with Print to Passbook or later with Update Passbook; a decrease prints as a payment and an increase negative, in the column it changes. | The passbook's balance must follow the loan; "Print to Passbook" on the other forms prints the transaction's line now, and Update Passbook prints what is left. The negative sign is the one an error correction already uses. |
| D2 | The adjustments recorded before version 37 are marked as passed over once by the migration (not printed, on no page). | They were never meant for the passbook: printing them now would put old lines after newer ones in a printed passbook. A reset of the print line to an earlier date brings them back with the rest. |
| D3 | The borrower list is fetched from the server as the box is typed in (50 at a time, with the total), not written into the page. | A cooperative has thousands of loans; the list stays quick and follows the branch scope of the user. |
| D4 | A batch is computed straight into review; Validate is now "Finish the review"; approval by another user with the resolution and posting stay. | The workbook asks for a review before posting; the approval with the Board / General Assembly resolution by someone other than the preparer was a requirement of the year-end distribution (RA 9520) and is kept. |
| D5 | "Owes the cooperative" shows the past-due amortizations and penalties of open loans, a hold, the loan balance and the unpaid subscription; a past-due amount or a hold marks the member. | These are what the cooperative's own ledgers know of a member's obligations; the reviewer decides, with the reason recorded. |
| D6 | A member removed stays on the batch (greyed, with the reason) and can be put back; the printed register lists them under Not included. | The review is part of the record: who was left out and why. |
| D7 | The sample database has an Interest on Share Capital batch in review, the two members with a past-due loan removed. | It shows the review screen without computing one. |
