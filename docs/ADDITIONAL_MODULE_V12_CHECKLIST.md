## 2. Decisions

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | The computation belongs to the rate code, not to the whole utility. | U85 asks for an option "in the computation of water billing"; a rate code is where the tiers are, and a cooperative can then bill one class by ladder and another straight. "Applicable for all water billing" is met by computing every bill through the one function that reads the rate code. |
| D2 | Every rate code on file stays Straight Rate Billing. | U83: the straight bill is "acceptable if the company used Straight Rate Billing"; no posted or upcoming bill changes until someone chooses the ladder. |
| D3 | On the ladder, a FIXED first tier is a minimum charge for its range; a later FIXED tier adds its amount once reached. | T66: 0-5 is 145.50 whatever the consumption in it; the same reading as the straight rate's minimum charge. |
| D4 | The free cubic meters of a discount come off the top of the consumption, then the rest is priced by the computation. | The same order as the straight rate; on the ladder the member saves the dearest cubic meters. |
| D5 | A posted bill keeps the computation it was made with. | Bills are snapshots: changing a rate code later must not change what was billed. |
| D6 | Several charges make several bills, one per charge, in one all-or-none transaction. | Each charge keeps its own bill code, accounts, discount and stock, as a single charge does; one refused charge must not leave half the form posted. |
| D7 | A new category or date issued voids the invoice and posts a corrected one, only while nothing is collected. | The category decides the accounts and the date the period of the invoice; the water rules already allow replacing an uncollected bill and refuse it once collected. |
| D8 | The receipt amounts are protected by a special permission, not by a page right. | The cashier must still open and print the receipt; only changing its amounts is restricted. |
| D9 | With times, a receipt counts at the moment it was recorded; a period of whole days counts by business date. | Q237: "only collection within the specified DateTime period"; the only time a receipt has is when it was recorded. The report has always counted whole days by business date (a receipt entered after midnight for the day before, or back-dated, belongs to its business day), and does so still when no time is narrowed. |
| D10 | Changing the category or the date issued takes the Edit right on One-Time Billing. | It voids an invoice and posts another; issuing bills (Create) is a different duty. |
| D11 | A change of a rate code's computation applies to the bills computed from then on; the readings saved but not posted keep their amount, and the message on saving says how many. | The same as a change of the tariff: a saved reading carries the amount computed when it was saved, and is computed again when it is saved again. |
| D12 | The water pages stop, as the other pages do, until the migration is run. | Without the column the bills cannot be computed; the home page names the file to run. |
