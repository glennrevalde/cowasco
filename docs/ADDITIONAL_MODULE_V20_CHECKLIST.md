## 2. Decisions

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | One page for the four billing types, one line per balance in the review. | A member's balances of several types come from one file; a line per balance can be corrected or deleted on its own (a wrong installment balance does not take the member's other balances with it). |
| D2 | The recurring, installment and one-time balances are invoices against 39999 Legacy Water Balances Clearing. | They are opening balances: the income was recognised in the former books. It is how the migration from the former water program records the open bills; the receivables are on the ledger and in the subledger at once, and the cashier collects them as any bill. |
| D3 | A recurring balance of a member not enrolled in the plan goes on an ended subscription. | A recurring bill needs a plan and a subscription; an ended one holds the balance without billing the member from now on (enrolling the member on Recurring Billing does that). |
| D4 | A fixed share capital balance needs the member on Fixed Share Capital Billing. | A share capital bill belongs to a member's fixed billing (its amount, frequency and share capital account); inventing one would bill the member monthly an amount nobody chose. The line says to put the member on fixed billing first (Add member or Import) and Check again. |
| D5 | An installment balance is a new contract dated the bill date, in equal installments from the due date given. | The remaining installments are what the member still pays; the contract is listed on the statements from the bill date (the first installment left may be in arrears). |
| D6 | The same balance (account, billing type, charge category, date and amount) is refused when an earlier upload holds it and its bill is not cancelled. | A file uploaded twice would double the receivables. Another amount on the same date is allowed with a note. |
