## 2. Decisions

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | The term is kept on the billing and on each bill. | A bill keeps the due date it was posted with when the term of the billing is changed later. |
| D2 | A share capital bill past its due date is marked Late at the cashier, but no penalty or compromise is suggested. | AI12 and M21 ask for a due date; nothing asks for a penalty on a contribution (v13 D9 stays: it is not a receivable on the books). |
| D3 | A batch and an import are all or none. | A partly saved batch would leave the user to find which members are missing; the review shows every problem first. |
| D4 | One import page per kind, sharing one review grid (`import-review.js`). | The two imports have different columns and checks, but the user reviews, corrects and removes the same way. |
| D5 | The one-time import names the charge by the CODE NAME of Bill Codes and makes one bill and one invoice per line. | "multiple charges with different amount from many members": Issue Individual Charge makes one bill per charge, so the bills, invoices and statements look the same. |
| D6 | A blank cell takes the value of the form; a filled cell keeps its own; OPEN on a line means no due date. | A file may hold only the members and their amounts (the workbook's format) or every value per line. |
| D7 | Excel .xlsx is read without a library; the old .xls is refused with a message to save as .xlsx or CSV. | XAMPP has ZipArchive and XML; .xls needs a library the package does not carry. |
| D8 | The stock of a material is checked over all the lines in file order. | Two lines of the same material must not take more than the stock together; the line that goes past it is the one to correct. |
| D9 | The review checks what posting checks: the class (above the lines), the branch, a closed period, the A/R and income accounts of the bill code, the stock, at most 2,000 lines. | A line shown ready must post; a refusal at Post would leave the user guessing. |
| D10 | A water account is named by its account number; its account ID is taken only for an account that has no number (with a note). A number Excel stripped of its leading zero is put back only when one account fits. | A mistyped number must never land on another member's account. |
| D11 | Only the usual date forms are read (2027-01-31, 01/31/2027, 31-Jan-2027, Jan 31, 2027 ...); an amount is at most 999,999,999.99. | A word or a year alone is not a date; a figure beyond the bills' columns would be cut silently. |
| D12 | The accounts of a batch or import, and the members, bill codes and stock of a one-time import, are locked first, in one order, before anything is read. | Two users posting at the same time must never bill an account twice or sell stock that is gone. |
