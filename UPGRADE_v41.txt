G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 41 (2026-11-01)
=================================================================

The requests of ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_WATER_COOP_SYSTEMS_v12.xlsx (Water Billing). This
package holds only the files added or changed since version 40. Copy the folder over your
installation, keeping the folder structure. config/config.php is not included and is
never changed.

Database
--------
One migration, for MySQL 5.5 (back up the database first):

  database/migrations/2027_03_07_water_ladder_billing.sql

  phpMyAdmin: click the database > Import > choose the file > Go
  or:         php tools/upgrade.php

It adds one column, rate_code.billing_method (the computation of a rate code). Every
rate code on file keeps Straight Rate Billing, so no bill changes until a rate code is
set to Ladder Rate Billing. Nothing is deleted. Safe to run a second time. Until it is
run, the program (the water pages too) stops after log-in with a page that names it.

database/accounting_system.sql and accounting_system_clean.sql are written again with
the column (a new installation needs nothing else).

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the changed
water/common/ui-consistency.css.

What changed (see docs/ADDITIONAL_MODULE_V12_CHECKLIST.md)
----------------------------------------------------------
- Bill Readings, individual: the member search answered "Member search failed" for
  every search (the batch reading lists members by street and was not affected). It
  finds the member again, by any part of the name in any order ("maria santos",
  "santos, maria"), the account number, the account ID or the street, and shows the
  account number and address. The same search on Generate Statement of Account
  (members did not appear) is fixed with it.
- Rate Codes: a Computation for each rate code, chosen when it is added or edited:
    Straight Rate Billing  the whole consumption at the rate of the tier it falls in
                           (250 m3 x 55.40 = 13,850.00), as before;
    Ladder Rate Billing    each tier's own cubic meters at that tier's rate, added up
                           (145.50 + 5 x 31.80 + 10 x 34.30 + 10 x 44.80 + 10 x 50.10
                           + 210 x 55.40 = 13,230.50).
  It applies to every water bill of the rate code's accounts: individual and batch
  readings and their live preview, Post Billing, bill corrections and statements. The
  list of rate codes shows each computation and what 30 m3 come to under both; Water
  Meter Rates shows it beside the tiers. A posted bill keeps the computation it was
  made with; a reading saved but not posted keeps its amount until it is saved again
  (the message on saving the rate code says how many there are).
- One-Time Billing, Issue Individual Charge: several charge categories on one form.
  Tick each charge, type its amount (a material: its quantity, at its sales price),
  find a charge by typing; the total is shown under the list. One bill and one
  invoice is made for each charge, all or none.
- One-Time Billing, Update Bill Record: the Charge Category and the Date Issued can be
  changed. The bill is issued again: its invoice is voided (kept, marked replaced) and
  the corrected invoice "<no>-R1" posted on the date issued with the new category's
  accounts (a material's stock follows). Allowed while nothing is collected on the
  bill (an amount lowered before is not a collection: its credit memo goes with the
  invoice); a paid bill shows them locked and the server refuses them. It takes the
  Edit right on One-Time Billing. The amount on the form is before the member
  discount, which is applied again on saving.
- Generate Official Receipt: the Paid Bill-Code Amounts and the Total Amount are
  changed before printing only by a user whose role has the new special permission
  "Water Billing: can change the paid bill-code amounts and the total on an official
  receipt before printing it" (User Role Configuration; administrators
  always). For the others they are read-only.
- Collection Summary (Water Billing > Reports): From and To are a date and a time; only
  the receipts recorded within that period are listed and totalled. The receipts show
  the date and time they were recorded beside their business date. A period of whole
  days (12:00 AM to 11:59 PM, and the presets) counts by business date, as before.
- Disconnection Management: the page no longer slides under the menu.
- Icons of the edit and delete buttons visible again on the water lists; a long choice
  in a water drop-down is cut with "..." instead of pushing the field wider.

Set-up after the upgrade
------------------------
- For a cooperative that bills by ladder: Water Billing > Rate Codes > edit the rate
  code > Computation: Ladder Rate Billing.
- To let a cashier change the printed receipt amounts: User Role Configuration > the
  role > the special permission above.
