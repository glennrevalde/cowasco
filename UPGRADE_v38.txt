G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 38 (2027-02-14)
=================================================================

The requests of ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_WATER_COOP_SYSTEMS_v9.xlsx. This package holds
only the files added or changed since version 37. Copy the folder over your
installation, keeping the folder structure. config/config.php is not included and
is never changed.

Database
--------
Nothing to run: this version adds no migration and changes no table. The Delete
right it uses on Interest on Share Capital and Patronage Refund is already given
to the General Manager and the Branch Manager roles.

database/accounting_system.sql and accounting_system_clean.sql are not changed.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the new
assets/js and assets/css files (assets/js/dist_batch.js is new).

What changed (see docs/ADDITIONAL_MODULE_V9_CHECKLIST.md)
---------------------------------------------------------
- Interest on Share Capital and Patronage Refund, where each amount comes from:
  point at a member's amount in the Basis column (or tap it on a phone, or Tab
  onto it) and a window shows how it is worked out.
    Average daily balance: each stretch of days the share capital balance stood
    the same (dates, days, balance with what changed it, balance x days), the
    total, and "2,421,400.00 / 302 days = 8,017.88".
    Ending balance: the balance brought forward, each change, the balance at the
    end of the period.
    Patronage refund: the documents behind the amount (date, document no. that
    opens it, amount), grouped by income account, and their total.
  Then how the member's amount comes from it: the basis x the rate, or the amount
  to distribute x the member's basis / the basis of the members on the list, the
  rounding, the adjustment and the net. If an entry dated in the period was
  recorded or voided after the batch was computed, the window says so (Recompute
  takes it).
- The totals under the list of members: Basis, Computed, Adjustment and Net of
  the members on the list; the basis of the members removed; and, while Find a
  member or "Only those with a past-due loan or on hold" narrows the list, the
  totals of the members shown.
- Deleting a computation never posted: Delete on the batch (title bar) and beside
  each batch on Distribution Batches, for a batch in review, reviewed or approved.
  A window names the batch and asks the reason; the batch and its list of members
  are removed, the audit trail keeps them as they were with the reason, and the
  batch number is not used again. A posted batch is reversed instead (as before).
  Rights: Delete on the page (Interest on Share Capital or Patronage Refund); an
  approved batch also needs Approve.

Set-up after the upgrade
------------------------
Nothing is required. To let another role delete unposted batches, give it the
Delete right on Interest on Share Capital and Patronage Refund (System
Administration > User Role Configuration).
