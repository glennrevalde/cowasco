G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 45 (2026-11-02)
=================================================================

A correction of version 44. This package holds only the files changed since version 44.
Copy the folder over your installation, keeping the folder structure. config/config.php
is not included and is never changed. No migration: nothing to run on the database.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser.

What changed
------------
- A water-service account with no first name showed no name ("—") on Reading Entry
  (Recorded Readings) and on several other water lists. Such an account is an
  organization of the former water program (182 of them, e.g. a municipality or a
  church), or the account of a joint account or an institution opened from Member
  Information > Water Defaults: its whole name is in the last name. The lists joined
  "last name, first name", and the database gives no name at all when one part is
  missing. Every list now shows the last name alone when there is no first name:
  Reading Entry, Reading History, Batch reading, Generate SOA / Post Billing, Billing
  List, the Water Dashboard, Collections (Cashier) and its searches, Payment History,
  One-Time, Installment and Recurring Billing, Discounted Members, the water search and
  the water reports. The receipts and notices print the name without a stray comma.
- "Acct#" on Reading Entry, "Acct:" on Reading History and on Generate SOA / Post
  Billing, and "Account #" on the reading form showed the account's internal ID (37)
  instead of its account number (50037). They show the account number. Reading History
  also finds an account by its account number.
