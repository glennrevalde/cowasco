G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 42 (2026-11-01)
=================================================================

The requests of ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_WATER_COOP_SYSTEMS_v13.xlsx. This package holds only
the files added or changed since version 41. Copy the folder over your installation,
keeping the folder structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
One migration, for MySQL 5.5 (back up the database first):

  database/migrations/2027_03_14_share_capital_billing.sql

  phpMyAdmin: click the database > Import > choose the file > Go
  or:         php tools/upgrade.php

It adds three tables (share_capital_plans, share_capital_bills, share_capital_collections),
one column payment_transactions.share_capital_amount (0.00 on every receipt on file), the
payment and item type "sharecap", and the page Water Billing > Billing > Fixed Share
Capital Billing, after One-Time Billing, given to the roles that have One-Time Billing
with the same rights. Nothing is deleted and no amount changes. Safe to run a second
time. Until it is run, the program (the water pages too) stops after log-in with a page
that names it.

database/accounting_system.sql and accounting_system_clean.sql are written again with
it (a new installation needs nothing else).

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser.

What changed (see docs/ADDITIONAL_MODULE_V13_CHECKLIST.md)
----------------------------------------------------------
- Inventory Center, transaction history: "On hand after" and "Avg after" are worked out
  in the order of the transaction dates. A transaction recorded later with an earlier
  date (back-dated) takes its place and the balances after it are right; the top line
  is the quantity on hand. Nothing stored changes.
- Water Billing > Billing > Fixed Share Capital Billing (new):
    Transaction code upon collection  a savings deposit code (CSD Cash Savings Deposit
                                      until another is chosen) and its Class
    Members on fixed billing          Member name, Share capital account (open share
                                      capital accounts only), Frequency (monthly,
                                      quarterly, semi-annually, annually), Start date,
                                      Posting day (1 to 31), Amount; Edit, Hold /
                                      Resume, Delete, and the Bills of each member
  A bill is posted on its posting day (the last day of a shorter month), with NO journal
  entry: it only tracks the contribution still unpaid. Hold stops the bills until
  Resume (the days on hold are not billed); Delete cancels the unpaid bills. An edit starts
  after the period already billed (nothing is billed twice or back-billed). A closed
  account or a member who left is not billed.
- Collections (Cashier): the share capital bills are open items of the member's
  water-service account (the same person). Collected, each is a deposit to the share
  capital account by the code of the page: Dr the cashier's cash, Cr share capital, a
  row of the Savings Account Transaction (savings number series, the receipt as OR)
  ready for Passbook Printing. The receipt shows FIXED SHARE CAPITAL; Collection
  Corrections reverses it with ECSD (the deposit cannot be deleted or corrected on its own
  in the savings module). The product's minimum deposit does not apply to these
  contributions; they are never overdue (no penalty on them).
- Collection Summary: Report type Summary (as before, with a Share capital column) or
  Detailed (a column for each code name collected, never put together). Water Reports >
  Collection & Payments and Payment History show the share capital too.

Set-up after the upgrade
------------------------
- Water Billing > Billing > Fixed Share Capital Billing > Collection: check the
  transaction code (CSD) and choose the class, then Save.
- Add the members (Add member). A member needs an open share capital account; the
  cashier collects on the member's water-service account, the one opened under the
  member's party (Water Service Accounts > Add, the party chosen). The page flags a
  member with no water-service account.
- Optional: a daily scheduled task, php water/process/runShareCapitalBilling.php, posts
  the bills of the day early in the morning (they are posted anyway when the page or the
  cashier opens).
