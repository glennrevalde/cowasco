G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 40 (2027-02-28)
=================================================================

The requests of ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_WATER_COOP_SYSTEMS_v11.xlsx, and the checker
asked for with it (the rows of an upload in red). This package holds only the files
added or changed since version 39. Copy the folder over your installation, keeping
the folder structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
Nothing to run: this version adds no migration, no table and no column (it runs on
MySQL 5.5 as before). database/accounting_system.sql and
accounting_system_clean.sql of version 39 are still current.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the new
assets/js/csv_review.js and assets/css/app.css.

What changed (see docs/ADDITIONAL_MODULE_V11_CHECKLIST.md)
----------------------------------------------------------
- Import Loans (Loan Accounts Center title bar), for the move from the former
  system. The file has one row per loan as it was released: Member Code, Member
  Name, Loan Name, Principal Loan, Terms (months or days), Mode of Payment,
  Amortization Type, Interest Rate, Release Date, Principal Amount Paid, Interest
  Paid, Penalties Paid, Cut-off Date, and if wished Past Due Interest and Overdue
  Penalties (and the first due date, the loan no., the PN no., the accrued
  interest). A CSV template is downloadable. On the page: the Balancing GL account
  (the other side of the loans' journal entry, normally Opening Balance Equity)
  and the cut-off date.
    Each loan is rebuilt from its release: its amortization schedule, then the
    totals paid laid on it as if paid on the due dates of the amortizations, in
    order. When the file does not give them, the past due interest of a loan that
    has matured by the cut-off date (on the principal unpaid, from the maturity
    date, at the loan's rate) and the overdue penalties (by the product's penalty
    terms on the past-due amortizations, less the penalties paid) are worked out.
    The rows are reviewed before the upload as on the other imports. The upload
    makes every loan Active (Current or Past due), with its schedule, a loan ledger
    from its release (the payments of the former system on their due dates, the
    past due interest and penalties at the cut-off date) and one journal voucher
    per cut-off date and balancing account: Dr the loan receivable of each
    product for the principal balances (and Loan Interest Receivable for an
    accrued interest given), Cr the balancing account. The payments of the former
    system are not collections: they are not on Loan Collection, the Loan
    Collections report or Home's "collected today".
  Reading and checking a file needs the Create right on Loan Accounts Center;
  uploading needs Approve.
- Import Member: "Full template (every detail)" gives a template with every other
  detail of the member too (53 more columns: salutation, place of birth, the
  provincial and office addresses, education and work, PMES date, subscribed
  shares, cluster, account officer and the other groupings, landline, alert and
  notes, the spouse, two beneficiaries). Keep only the columns you have; the
  review shows only those the file fills.
- The checker on every upload page (Import Member, Import Savings, Import Time
  Deposit, Import Loans, Batch Deposit and Withdrawal, Import items): a row with a
  problem is shown in red, with its messages and the cells they name outlined; it
  is checked again by itself as it is corrected, and "Remove the rows with a
  problem" takes every red row out at once (Put back the last brings them back).
- Loan numbers: a number already on file (a loan of the former system imported
  with its own number) is passed over when a loan is numbered.
- Loan ledger: the entries are named as on the other loan pages ("Interest
  accrual", "Write-off", "Error correction", "Past due interest" ...) on the loan
  page, the Statement of Account, the Member Statement and the voucher's member
  ledgers.

Set-up after the upgrade
------------------------
Nothing is required. Before importing loans: the loan products of the former
system on Loan Products (with their penalty terms and "Additional interest when
past due"), and the members imported.
