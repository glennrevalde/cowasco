G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 33 (2027-01-10)
=================================================================

The requests of ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_WATER_COOP_SYSTEMS_v5.xlsx. This package holds
only the files added or changed since version 32. Copy the folder over your
installation, keeping the folder structure. config/config.php is not included and
is never changed.

Database
--------
Run ONE new migration on your database (phpMyAdmin: select the database > Import;
Navicat: Execute SQL File; or php tools/upgrade.php), after backing it up:

    database/migrations/2027_01_10_teller_menu_loan_release_vouchers.sql

It:
- moves Loan Collection and the Savings Transaction Center to the Teller menu
  (their page keys and the rights of every role stay as they are);
- adds the page Loans > Loan Release Vouchers, with the rights the roles have on
  Loans > Loan Release;
- adds loan_accounts.voucher_no (the voucher ID of a loan release, LR- series),
  filled in for the loans released before, and an index on release_txn_id;
- adds loan_products.addl_interest (how the Internal Ledger computes additional
  interest on a past-due loan; "auto" for every product);
- adds the number series "Loan release voucher ID" (LR-000001) if it is missing.

Nothing is deleted, no posted amount changes. Written for MySQL 5.5, safe to run
twice. With several companies, run it on every company database (Super Admin:
Companies > Database updates > Update all companies). Until it is run, the
program shows a page naming it.

database/accounting_system.sql and accounting_system_clean.sql (new installs only)
include the change. Do NOT import those two files over a live database.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the new
assets/js and assets/css files.

Set-up after the upgrade
------------------------
1. Chart of Accounts: the Cash/bank Account of the Loan Release Form lists the
   active accounts of type Bank. An account with its bank account no. filled in,
   or "Bank" in its name (Cash in Bank - BDO Checking), is offered for a Cheque
   release; the others (Cash on Hand, Revolving Fund) for a Cash release. If your
   chart does not tell them apart, every Bank-type account is offered for both.
2. Class List: every loan release voucher carries a class, chosen on the form
   (the class of the loan application is proposed). Make sure the classes exist.
3. Cooperative > Document Numbering: "Loan release voucher ID" gives the voucher
   ID printed on the Cash / Check Voucher (LR-000001 onwards; the next number
   continues after the loans already released). Change the prefix if needed.
4. Loans > Loan Products, each product: "Additional interest when past due
   (Internal Ledger)": By the interest method (the default), On the principal of
   each missed amortization, On the principal balance after maturity, or None.
5. User Role Configuration: Loans > Loan Release Vouchers has the rights of Loan
   Release. Give View to the cashier and the bookkeeper if they should see the
   list.
6. A teller who releases a loan in cash from his own drawer must have his shift
   open; the release is then a cash-out of the shift. A release from another cash
   account (Revolving Fund) needs no shift.

What changed (see docs/ADDITIONAL_MODULE_V5_CHECKLIST.md)
--------------------------------------------------------
- Teller menu: Loan Collection and the Savings Transaction Center are now under
  Teller (no longer under Loans and Savings & Deposits).
- Daily Collection & Disbursement Summary: every transaction of the teller on
  Loan Collection (payments and their error corrections) and on the Savings
  Transaction Center (deposits, withdrawals and their error corrections), the
  official receipts, the loan releases paid from the drawer and the member
  disbursements, by date and teller, with the Cash, Check and Other columns, the
  cash paid out and the net cash; "One line per day and teller" summarises.
- Member Disbursement: choosing a member works again (the member's details show).
- Loans > Loan Release opens the Loan Release Form of the former program: Member
  Code and Name, Loan Code, P.N No, Loan Name, Payment Details (Installment Mode
  Fixed Principal / Equal Loan, Term, Mode of Payment, Interest Rate, Start Payment
  Date, Due Date), Release Date, Net Proceeds (Approved Amount), Release Type
  (Cash or Cheque, the Cheque No.), Cash/bank Account, Co-Makers and the Loan
  Charges grid (Charge Name, Charge Account, Rate/Amount, Calculated Amount, Total
  Charges). Add deduction / delete a row; a rate or an amount can be changed; the
  net proceeds follow as you type. After the release the same form shows the loan
  with Print To Passbook, Disbursement, Promissory, Disclosure, Amortization,
  Co-Maker and Loan Release Stmt.
- The release is a Banking transaction: a Cash release is a Cash Voucher of
  Banking > Cash Voucher, a Cheque release a check of Banking > Write Checks
  numbered with the cheque no., both with the class and the voucher ID. They are
  not edited or deleted in Banking: void the release (the loan is cancelled with
  it). The voucher prints in the format of the former program (Voucher ID, Date,
  Paid to, Check no., Account ID, Account Name, Description, SL Code, Debit,
  Credit, Prepared / Paid / Received / Approved / Checked by).
- Loans > Loan Release Vouchers: every voucher of a loan release (Cash Voucher,
  Check Voucher and the loan release vouchers of before), with filters, search,
  print, export and Open in Banking.
- Loan Release Statement: the format of the former program, without the
  amortization schedule: the cooperative's name, address and contact number; the
  member, address, loan type, mode of payment, date released, P.B. No. (the
  member's passbook no.), terms, interest rate, total interest, date due; the loan
  release less the deductions and the net loan proceeds; the amortization by year
  (principal, interest, total); Prepared By (who processed the release), Approved
  By (who approved it) and Loan Proceeds Received By.
- Loans > Loan Accounts Center is the center of the former program: Collection,
  Ledger, Internal Ledger, View Schedule, Update Passbook, Refresh, Filter, Print
  and Search; the columns Account No., Member name, Loan Name, Release Date, Due
  Date, Principal Loan, Principal Balance, Interest Balance, Current Balance.
- Loan Ledger Form (Ledger): Date, Reference No, Release, Prin. Payments, Prin.
  Balance, Int. Payments, Fines/Surc. and the totals; Passbook View or View All;
  printable.
- Internal Ledger Form: the ledger by principal, interest and penalty (Release /
  Additional Charges, Payments, Balances) with the additional interest and the
  penalty the loan has earned to a date when it is past due or an amortization is
  missed, by the loan product's set-up; printable. These computed rows are shown,
  not posted: to collect them, raise the interest with Loans > Loan Adjustments
  and use Override Payment on Loan Collection; the penalty is charged when the
  loan is collected, as before.
- Loans > Loan Products: "Additional interest when past due (Internal Ledger)".
