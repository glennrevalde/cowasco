G-SMaRT Accounting - version 26 (2026-11-01)
=============================================

This package holds only the files that were added or changed since version 25.
Copy the accounting-system folder over your installation, keeping the folder
structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
Run ONE new migration on your database (phpMyAdmin: select the database > Import;
Navicat: Execute SQL File), after backing it up:

    database/migrations/2026_11_01_cooperative.sql

It adds the Cooperative Management System: its tables; three empty columns
(users.coop_branch_id, users.coop_cash_account_id, other_names.member_id); the
cooperative accounts of the chart where their codes are still free (1110, 1210-1230,
2300-2370, 3010-3340, 4400-4480, 7100-7120) and the Cooperative group of Module
Default Accounts; the six new menus and their pages; eight roles (General Manager,
Bookkeeper, Loan Officer, Credit Committee, Teller, Membership Officer, Collector,
Branch Manager) with their page rights and special permissions, and cooperative
rights for the Accountant and Auditor roles; number series, savings transaction
codes, member types, passbook types, CDA field mappings, a regulatory calendar and
policy defaults. Nothing is deleted and no amount changes. Written for MySQL 5.5,
safe to run twice. With several companies, run it on every company database (Super
Admin: Companies > Database updates > Update all companies). Until it is run, the
program shows a page naming it.

After running it, open Chart of Accounts > Module Default Accounts, Cooperative
group: where your chart already used one of the codes above, the cooperative default
points at your own account with that code. Correct it before posting.

database/accounting_system.sql and accounting_system_clean.sql (new installs only)
include the change; the first also has a sample cooperative (two branches, 12
members, savings, time deposits and loans, and the cooperative users with the
password Demo@12345: manager, bookkeeper, branchmgr, loanofficer, creditcom,
membership, teller1, teller2). Do NOT import those two files over a live database.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the new
assets/js/coop.js and assets/css/app.css.

What is new (the requests of "PH_Cooperative_Management_System_Complete_Features"
and "ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_COOP_SYSTEMS_v1.xlsx")
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Cooperative: Member Center and Member Information (full profile, joint and
    institutional members, documents with expiry, photo and signature,
    beneficiaries, status workflow with reasons, holds and alerts, attendance,
    notes); Member Statement of Account with the member ID card, the share capital
    certificate and the certificate of balances; Cooperative Vouchers; Opening
    Balances Import (members, savings and share capital, time deposits, loans:
    checked first, all or nothing); set-up of the profile and policies, branches,
    lists, passbook types, document numbering and document wording.
 2. Savings & Deposits: products with effective-dated rate tiers; deposits,
    withdrawals and transfers with the approval rules; interest posting and
    dormancy; passbook printing with line tracking, cover and balance forwarded;
    time deposits (placement, certificate, monthly accrual, maturity, renewal,
    pre-termination); payroll / CBU batch deductions; check clearing.
 3. Loans: products and charges; applications with the eligibility check, credit
    investigation, approval by level; release (cash, cheque or credit to savings,
    with the payoff of a renewed loan); promissory note, disclosure statement with
    the effective interest rate, schedule, co-maker statement, statement of account,
    reminders and demand letters; collection with the penalty, interest and
    principal allocation; loan accounts; adjustments; waivers; renewal,
    restructuring, write-off and recovery; penalty and interest accrual runs;
    aging by maturity, at PAR and by amortization.
 4. Teller: shifts with the cash count by denomination and the printed cash count
    sheet, official receipts and member disbursements (including the settlement of
    members who have resigned, been terminated or died).
 5. Year-end Distribution: net surplus allocation to the statutory funds, interest
    on share capital and patronage refund batches with preview, approval by another
    user, posting and reversal.
 6. CDA Compliance: officers and committees, compliance tracking, regulatory
    calendar, CAPR data preparation and CSV export with an editable field mapping.
 7. 47 cooperative reports, among them the Statement of Financial Condition,
    Statement of Operations, Statement of Changes in Equity and the Subsidiary vs
    General Ledger Reconciliation.
 8. The Home page shows the cooperative figures and what needs attention; the top
    bar shows the count.

Every money event posts one balanced voucher to the same general ledger; nothing
posted is edited or deleted (void with a reason, or an adjusting entry).

Documentation and tests
-----------------------
- The built-in User's Manual has a new chapter 13, Cooperative Management; every
  cooperative page's Help button opens its section. The Word edition of the manual
  does not include it yet.
- docs/COOP_IMPLEMENTATION_NOTES.md: installing, setting up, routines, how the
  module posts, formulas, assumptions and limitations.
- docs/COOP_REQUIREMENTS_CHECKLIST.md: every requirement with where it is and its
  status (Done / Partial / Not done).
- tests/coop/: automated tests (php tests/coop/run.php, on a throw-away database)
  and their latest results in tests/coop/RESULTS.md.
