G-SMaRT Integrated Cooperative System - version 48 (2026-11-07)
=================================================================

A correction of the platform (several companies). This package holds only the files
changed since version 47. Copy the folder over your installation, keeping the folder
structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed. No migration:
nothing to run on the database.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser.

What changed
------------
- Platform > New company could not finish: every new company failed at the last step
  with "The new database does not match the program: 2026_12_01_water_billing.sql,
  2027_01_24_account_types.sql (bring the template company up to date first)", and the
  half-made database was removed. The template was not the cause. The set-up copied
  every table of the template but not the water module's two views (chartofaccntstbl
  and operator), nor the account types (every account of the copied chart of accounts
  points at one) or the business units (the Water Utility among them), and the check at
  the end found them missing. A new company now receives:
    - the water module's two views (the database user of the program needs the CREATE
      VIEW right, as it already needs CREATE, DROP and TRIGGER for a new company);
    - besides the set-up lists copied before: the account types, the business units
      (without their usual site, since the template's sites are not copied), the
      cooperative's lists (member types, savings transaction codes, passbook types and
      their designs, ID types, documents, loan purposes, collateral types, account
      classes, the CDA report lines and calendar, address types, contact channels,
      cities) and the water billing set-up (its settings, the organization units, the
      bill codes, pipe sizes, rate codes and rates, meter discounts, discounts and their
      privileges, recurring billing plans, the Data Import Management profiles). An
      Inventory bill code comes without its item, since items are not copied: link it
      again on Bill Codes;
    - the water module's institution profile with its own name, address, phone, e-mail
      and TIN, and none of the template's registered cooperative name, CDA registration
      number and signatories (they were copied before).
  Still never copied: customers, suppliers, items, members, employees, savings and loan
  products, classes, users, transactions and any history.
- A company whose set-up failed: open it on Platform > Companies and press "Set it up
  again" (type the owner's account again if it is to be made with it). Nothing else is
  needed; the failed record does not have to be deleted.
- A template that still needs a program update is now refused before anything is made
  ("... needs a database update first (Database updates): ..."), instead of failing at
  the end.
- New company: the list of the copied set-up lists is grouped (Company and access,
  Accounting, Sales and inventory, Cooperative, Water billing), each group opened with
  a click.
- The platform pages on a phone: the top bar shows "G-SMaRT" and the Super Admin's
  icon on one line (the program's name ran into the account button and the page
  scrolled sideways).

Test: bash tests/platform/run.sh (a new company made from the clean database and checked
table by table; a failed company set up again; an out-of-date template refused).
